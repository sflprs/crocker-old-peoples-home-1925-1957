The Secretary then placed in nomination the names of the following ladies
to serve as the Board of Menagers for the ensuing year:

Mrse Jesse Andrews
Mrse Gilbert M. Barrett
Mrse George Le Bean
Mrs. Josephine P. Borda
Mrse Eo Te Cairns

Mrse George Re Gay
Mrse Le Oo Henderson
Mrge Re Ve Holder

Mrse John We. Leavitt
Miss Sarah Oddie

Mrse Charles Be Porter
Mrse Eo De Walkup

Miss Anna Willits

Mrse Eo We Wilson

The nominations were duly seconded, and there being no further nominations,
ballots were cast by the members present; whereupon the Secretary, reading from the
ballots, declared that the foregoing were duly elected to serve as the Board of
Managers for the ensuing year and until their successors are elected and duly qualified.

There being no further business, on motion, duly made and seconded, the
meeting adjourned.
