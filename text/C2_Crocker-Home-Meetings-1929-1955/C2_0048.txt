May 20, 1936+

ANNUAL MEETING

OLD PEOPLE'S HOME

In accordance with the By-Laws end pursuant to notice, duly gives
the annual meeting of the Old People's Home was called this day at 2:00 peme

After checking the number of members present, it was found that
there was not a quorum, However, the reports of the President, Secretary,

Treasurer, Room Committee, attorney and auditor for the Home were read and
approved and ordered filed.

After a general discussion the meeting adjourned.

Secretary
