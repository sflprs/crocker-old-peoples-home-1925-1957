an Orange Squeezer - Mrs Matson moved and Mrs Paddock seconded that an Orange Squeezer be purchased. Motion Carried.

In reference to Change of Butchers, Mrs Palache moved and Mrs Tufts seconded that. "The matter of dealing with the Trades people be brought up for discussion and decision with the Board of Trustees at the Annual meeting to be held on Wednesday, May 20th." The Secretary was instructed to inform the Supt. in writing of their action Motion carried -

In reference to the Baking. The question of whether it would be cheaper to buy bread than to continue to have it made by the Baker in the Home was discussed. Mrs Wilson moved and Mrs Matson seconded that "We try the bread supplied by the Bakery during the vacation of the Home Baker before making a final decision". Motion carried -

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.

                        Josephine P. Borda -
