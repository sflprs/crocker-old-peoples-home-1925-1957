Wednesday Feb 1-/28 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday Feb 1-/28

The [struck through: special] meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] —

Present Mmes. Dick Matson Palache Hoffmann Griffith Leavitt Borda Holden Wilcox Petersen Andrews Frink Plume [?] Tufts [?] McWilliams & Haven —

Secty's report was read accepted & filed —

Supt. Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read & accepted —

Treasurers report was read & on motion made by Mrs. Borda & sec. by Mrs. Matson it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee — The report from last month was found to be correct.

[In margin: Bal on hand 8663.50 — Disbursements 7301.61 — Bal on hand Jan 31 $14460.39 [?] —]

On motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Palache the Hospital Dept. is to have the privilege of providing 30.00 [?] a month for Mrs Andrus [?]

Applications —

Mrs Julia Voelmer [?] has finished her probationary time & on motion made by Mrs. Borda & sec. by Mrs Andrews she was accepted as a regular member of the House.

Margaretta Elliott [?] Gallagher [?] age 70 — she wishes a 3000.00 room & 150 — on motion made by Mrs. Frink & sec. by Mrs Plume [?] — she was accepted —

Ernestine Bourdillon [?] age 67 — she wishes to pay 3000 & the 150.00 but did not appear —

Hedwig [?] Carlsen [?] age 65 — wishes to pay 4000 & 150. but did not appear —

Mrs Malvina [?] Harmuth [?] came before the Board but did not wish to come into the Home until Oct. or Nov of this year.

On motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Petersen a letter is to be written by the Secty to the Board of Trustees asking what salary they consider giving the new Manager [?]

The lights in the living room are to be attended to — as the old people complain of the glare. on motion made by Mrs Frink & sec. by Mrs. Griffith —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday March /28 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
