The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday April 1-/31 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Mr. Dunham [?] —

Present Mmes. Leavitt Paddock Wilson Matson - Dick Walkup [?] Tufts Palache [?] Holden Andrews Bailhache [?] Gay Whicher [?] Borda [?]

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt. Matron & Head Nurses reports also read accepted & filed —

Treasurers report was also read Bal. on hand Feb 28/31 $2043.68 Disbursements for the month $6305.54 Bal. on hand March 31-/31 - $3238.14 on motion made by Mrs Palache [?] & sec. by Mrs Matson it was accepted & to be referred to auditing Committee - The reports for Dec. Jan & Feb. were found to be correct by the auditing Committee.

On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Bailhache [?] the bid for the painting of the dining room be given Mr. Weber [?] - the cost to be $150.00 —

Mrs Wilson & Mrs Bailhache [?] will confer together for the selection & purchasing of the wall paper & drapes for the dining room -

The kitchen & dining room committee are to investigate & make a report at the next meeting regarding the purchasing of bread instead of having it baked in the Home

On motion made by Mrs Whicher [?] & sec. by Mrs. Palache [?] the night nurses are to continue to have chops for their dinners until the hospital committee has consulted with the Head Nurse & Matron - The committee is also to confer with the Head Nurse & Matron & make up menus for the night workers & their meals —

The nominating committee was appointed for the selection of officers & members on the Board for the ensuing year - Mrs. Palache [?] named chairman to be assisted by Mrs. Bailhache [?] [struck through: Holden] Palache & McWilliams —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday May 6-/31 —
