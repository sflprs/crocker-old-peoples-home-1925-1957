Wednesday Oct 2- /29 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Oct 2-/29 — Meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham —

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen [?] Palache Haven Stevenson Borda - Matson Holden Leavitt Paddock —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read accepted & filed

Treasurer's report was read Bal. on hand Aug 31-/29 24762.87 Disbursements for the month 12754.14 Actual Bal. Sept 30 /29 20862.39 Savings Acct 20013.68 [?] [small figures written above: 7520.66 [?]] Supt's Bal 6.29 Checking acct 871.92 on motion made by Mrs. Borda & sec by Mrs. Palache it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

On motion made by Mrs. Leavitt & sec. by Mrs Matson the revolving fund to be created at the American Trust Bank on Divisadero St. for the use of the Supt. at the recommendation of the Board of Trustees - Secty to write Mr. Buckley [?] Secty of Trustees the motion to be enforced & carried out

Mr. Hickie's [?] report was read - & on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Holden, that the Board of Managers approve the method & stand ready to accept any financial arrangement made by the Trustees —

Applications

Mrs Rose Williams will finish her probation Oct 12-/29 — she was accepted as a regular member on motion made by Mrs. Leavitt, & sec. by Mrs. Haven —

Mrs Virginia [?] Post age 68 - she wishes to pay $3000 - 1000 for special care & 150.00 probation on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs Stevenson she was accepted —

Mrs. Eva C. Murray age 69 - she wishes to pay $3500 for her room & 1000.00 special care & 150 probation & as she cannot decide just when she could enter the Home she will pay 100.00 deposit for her room to be held until Oct 16-/29 - on motion made by Mrs. Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs. Holden she was accepted.

Mrs Celia [?] G. Parker age 78 - wishes to pay 3000 - 1000 for special care & 150 probation she was also accepted on motion made by Mrs. Paddock & sec. by Mrs. Stevenson —

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs. Haven the report for the service & new installation for the elevator be accepted. The new lavatory & details to be installed on the ground floor to be left to the advisory board on motion made by Mrs. Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs. Palache.

On motion made by Mrs. Borda & sec. by Mrs. Holden - the President Treasurer & Superintendent have been authorized to sign the card for the American Trust Bank for the revolving fund —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday November /29 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
