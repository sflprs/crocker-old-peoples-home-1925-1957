Wednesday Dec. 5-/28 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples House was held Wednesday Dec. 5-/28 —

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Wilson Leavitt Tufts [?] Andrews Palache Stephenson Paddock Plume [?] Wilcox Haven Matson & McWilliams & Borda

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr Dunham [?] —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read accepted & filed —

The Treasurers report was also read Bal. on hand Oct. 31-/28 — $48852.32 [?] Disbursements for the month $10366.41 [?] Actual balance $47868.00 [?] Nov. 30 /28 — Checking Acct 27195.93 [?] Savings 20672.07 [?]

Miss Jennie G. McGee & Mr Frank Wm Johnson will have finished their probation middle of this month & on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Andrews these are to be accepted as regular members —

Applications —

Mrs May Smith [?] age 67 — wishes to pay 3250.00 [?] & 150.00 probation & $1000.00 at 4% for extra care — she was accepted by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Tufts [?] —

Mrs. Grace Belle Kiddock [?] age 65 — she wishes to pay $3500.00 & 150.00 probation & 1000.00 for extra care. she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs Plume [?] providing Dr. Rummell's [?] recommendation was satisfactory — $150.00 will be added for burial — (suggested by Mrs. Hayes [?]) —

Mrs Annie Peters [?] age 77 — she wishes to pay 3000.00 & 150.00 as she would not state her finances she was refused acceptance.

Wm Smith Gray [?] age 71 — wishes to pay 4000 & 150 — as he did not appear he was not discussed —

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Andrews to call in the Superintendent Mr. Dunham [?] to discuss conditions existing of the trouble between Mrs Stump our matron & Mrs. Rhodes [?] the head nurse —

On motion made by Mrs. McWilliams & sec. by Mrs. Palache Mrs Dick & Mrs Hodgen have an interview with Mrs. Stump. —

& on motion made by Mrs. Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Matson, Mrs. Stump is to be given 2 mos vacation & 1 month pay — for absolute rest & recuperation, to be done as soon as she can get ready

The committee referred the compiling of the new contract to the Board of Managers & it is now to be submitted to our Atty — Mr. Flaherty [?] —

On motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs Leavitt, Mrs. Rhodes [?] hospital bill to be paid in full —

Dr Rummell [?] is to receive an additional fee for his Xmas gift owing to his kindness & extra care during the epidemic & to receive 50.00 instead of 20.00 as in former years — on motion made by Mrs. Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Palache —

Mention was also made of the Xmas box for donations from the members of the House for the employees, as was done last year — wreaths & greens to be ordered from the [illegible] as in former years —
