Wednesday March 6-/29 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday March 6-/29-

The meeting was opened with prayer Rev. Dunham —

Present Mmes Dick Hodgen [?] Wilson Borda, Matson Holden Paddock Andrews Palache Wilcox, Haven Leavitt. McWilliams

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read accepted & filed

Treasurers report was read Bal. on hand Jan 31st $50928.49 Bal. in both accounts — Disbursements for the month $11222.46 Bal. Feb. 28- $52022.61 checking acct $26964.69 — Savings acct $25057.92 — the auditing Committee found the reports for Dec /28 & Jan. /29 to be correct —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Andrews the suggestion made by Mrs Matson & accepted that Mr. Hall [?] our engineer is to look into the leaking of water into the basement then during the heavy rains - a siphon pump was suggested to take care of the water —

Applications —

Henry Barnett age 81 — wished to pay 3000 & 150 — but as his des [?] was for the Home to accept him without pay - he was not accepted —

George Dunes [?] age 69 — he also wishes to pay 3000 & 150 he did not appear - & his application was not read —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt that the head nurse be instructed in the future not to have any conversation with the members of the Home on financial matters - The motion was not carried —

Mrs. Cowden [?] came before the Board & her case was decided she will leave the Home on receiving her check refunding her the balance of the money left, after paying or rather deducting (15) fifteen months board at $75 per month — she will be allowed out of that time, 6 weeks she was out of the Home amounting to 112.50 — She is to receive 4 1/4 % on said money counted quarterly as would have been in the bank - Mr. Flaherty our Atty is to figure the Int. for her on the amount of $2487.50

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday April 3-/29 —

                        Respectfully submitted —
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams —
