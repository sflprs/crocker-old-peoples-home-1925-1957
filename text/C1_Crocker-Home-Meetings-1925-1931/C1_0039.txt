Wednesday — June 1-/27 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday June 1-/27 The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] — Present Mmes Dick Judson Tufts Andrews. Wilson Griffith Hoffmann Haven Leavitt Palache Holden Borda Bates Frink & Petersen & McWilliams Secty's report was read & accepted — Supt's & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed — Treasurers report — was read Bal. on hand April 30-/27 — $7857.84 Disbursements 6639.11 [?] Bal. on hand May 31-/27 — $11216.38 [?] on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Holden it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee — the report of last month was found to be correct —

Applications

Mrs. Lydia Manning age 67 — she wishes to pay $3500 & 150 — on motion made by Andrews & sec. by Mrs Haven [?] she was accepted — & on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Holden if the room was satisfactory to her, the probation time would be held over for 2 or 3 weeks & hold the room for her until then —

Anna R. Hackney [?] age 73. she wishes to pay 3500 & the 150.00 she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Holden & sec. by Mrs. Frink

Mrs. Elizabeth R. Garrick [?] age 73 — she wishes to pay 3000 & 150 as the conditions were not satisfactory she was not accepted —

Martin M. Bope [?] age 75 — he wishes to pay 3000 & 150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Griffith he was rejected —

Mrs Mary E. Barker 80 she had applied for a $3500.00 room & the $150.00 but decided on a 5000 room, she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs Borda —

The Board accepted with much regret the resignation of Mrs. Leavitt as chairman of the hospital committee —

A note of thanks is to be written Mr. Halliday who very kindly donated his work & made all the new racks for the books in the chapel —

Mary Elizabeth Petersen age 64 — wished to pay $3000 & 150.00 but as she is under age was told to return later —

On motion made by Mrs. Tufts & sec. by Mrs. Frink the head nurse & matron are each to have an increase of 25.00 in their salary making it $125.00 & to begin July 1st —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Aug /27

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams.

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs Griffith the members in the House must adhere to the rules of the laundry — allowing 6 pieces — The motion was made at the former meeting —
