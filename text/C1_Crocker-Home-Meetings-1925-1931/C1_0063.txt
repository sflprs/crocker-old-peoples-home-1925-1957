Wednesday Jan. 2-/29 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Jan 2-/29 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham [?] —

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Wilson Matson Stephenson Paddock Palache Holden Wilcox & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt. Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read accepted & filed —

Treasurers report also read — Bal on hand $47868.00 in both accounts, Disbursements for the month $8878.29 [?] Bal. on hand 45282.56 [?] checking acct $24667.09 [?] Savings acct $20672.07 [?], is to be referred to the auditing committee. The report from last month was found to be correct.

Miss E. F. Condit [?] Mrs. E. S. Teerilson [?] & Mr. J. C. Cunyer [?] have finished their probation & on motion made by Mrs. Matson & sec. by Mrs. Palache they were accepted as regular members —

Mrs. Emily Knight age 76 — wishes to pay $3000 & 150 — on motion made by Palache & sec. by Mrs. Wilcox she was accepted & without the 1000.00 for extra care in lieu of the fact that a friend will provide for her & cover all extra care expenses —

Mr. Wm Lange [?] age 69 — he also wishes to pay $3000 & 150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Matson & sec. by Mrs. Holden he was accepted & he will pay 1000.00 extra @ 4% for extra care.

Mrs. Alvida [?] Nelson age 73 — but did not appear —

Chas. G. [?] Halbeck [?] age 68 — will pay $3000 & 150.00 & $1000 for extra care & to be paid 4% he was accepted providing the doctors certificate is satisfactory —

Mrs Reimers [?] application was read again & will come before the Board next meeting —

On motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs Holden the letter from Mr. Bohner [?] probation [?] administrator to be answered & additional information he asks for be given him, & a copy of the By laws sent him giving our President the full right for signing all documents.

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Wilson in the case of returning to Mr. Kelly's nephew what is left of the $300.00 — it was decided it became property of the House when paid & could not be returned — a letter to be written to this effect —

A letter of thanks is to be written to Mrs C. C. Judson who donated the Xmas tree, also for all other donations —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Wilson Mrs. Martens [?] be allowed to have a reclining chair & will be permitted to return it to the owner Mrs. C. H. [?] Rushing [?] or her daughter when she no longer has need of it.

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. McWilliams Mrs Schroder [?] be asked to withdraw her resignation & stay on with the hope the trouble between her & Mrs Stump will be forgotten & on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Hodgen a letter to be written to her or Mrs McWilliams to have a talk with her.

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Feb. 6-/29 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        [first part of signature hidden by a fold in the page] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
