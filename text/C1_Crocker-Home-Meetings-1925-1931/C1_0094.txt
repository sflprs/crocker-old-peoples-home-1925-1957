Wednesday Feb. 4-/31 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Feb 4-/31 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham [?] —

Present Mmes. Leavitt Paddock Dick Wilson Palache [?] Holden Tufts Wilcox Haven [?] Stevenson Andrews Whicher [?] McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's Matron & Head Nurses reports read accepted & filed -

Treasurer's report also read. Bal. on hand Dec 31-/30 - $1964.88 Disbursements for the month 7489.27 [?] - Bal on hand Jan 31-/31 - 1975.54 [?] on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Palache [?] it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing committee.

A letter is to be written to Mrs. Cunningham advising her of the decision of the Board refusing her application to be allowed to leave the Home & the action of the Board has been sustained by the Board of Trustees - on motion made by Mrs Wilson & Mrs Tufts

Miss Julia Magee [?] has finished her probation on motion made by Mrs. Dick & sec. by Mrs Paddock she was accepted as a regular member of the Home.

Applications -

Mrs Eliza Benham age 84 - she wishes to pay $4000.00 on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Paddock she was accepted - but decided not to come in when she was told she had to pay 150.00 probation —

    Mrs Martha Fahey [?] age 67 wishes to pay $4000.00
    Hiram [?] Milton Hamilton age 78 -

they were not discussed as they did not appear —

On motion made by Mrs Matson & sec. by Mrs Palache [?] the office be requested to be more careful in filling out the applications & be more explicit regarding the admission fee - the minimum price being 4000 & 150.00 probation -

A letter to be written to Mr. Dunham [?] - [struck through: on motion made by]

The report given by Mrs Stevenson regarding the preparation of the [struck through: food] is to be referred to the kitchen & dining room Committee for action on motion made by Mrs. Tufts & sec. by Mrs. Wilcox —

The communication from Mrs. Stump [?] was read in which she tendered her resignation as Matron of the Old Peoples Home to take effect Feb. 4-/31 - on motion made by Mrs. Dick & sec. by Mrs. Wilcox it was accepted with regret — a letter giving her recommendation & commendation of her ability for the past six years in the Home & a guarantee of her salary to March first, to be sent her —

A letter is to be written to the Board of Trustees to have the contracts printed instead of type written on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Palache [?]
