Wednesday Jan 4-/28 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday Jan 4-/28

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?]

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Palache Borda Wilcox Wilson Tufts [?] Andrews Hoffmann Griffith Matson Frink Leavitt Stone [?] Bates Plume [?] & McWilliams.

Secty's report was read accepted & filed —

Supts Matrons & nurse's reports read & accepted —

Treasurers report was read Bal on hand Nov. 30-/27 $14519.74 Disbursements for the month $9989.79 [?] Bal on hand Dec 31 — $8663.50 [?] on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec by Mrs Matson it was accepted subject to the auditing Committee.

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Griffith, Mrs. Holland be required to pay 5.00 a month for special care & to be taken from her annuity if necessary — The order to be written to Dr. Morrill [?] so he will be informed & to collect.

The bill for the new nurse's room be sent to the Trustees & a very fitting note of thanks is to be written to Mr. McLean [?] for the time & interest he displayed in the building of it. on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Griffith

The letter from Dr. Stocking from Agnews State Asylum was read regarding Mrs Jennie Petersen, on motion made by Mrs Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Palache Dr. Morrill [?] is to write Dr. Stocking asking him to extend the leave of absence.

Applications —

Mrs. E. L. Miller has finished her probation time on motion made by Mrs. Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Griffith she was accepted

Mr. James Lynch age 78 — wishes to pay 3000 & 150.00 he was not accepted as the Board did not think him desirable

Mrs. Celia Oates [?] age 64 — she was not accepted as she was under age — on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Tufts [?] —

In the future there should always be two or more bids on any new work done in the House.

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Feb. 1 /28 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
