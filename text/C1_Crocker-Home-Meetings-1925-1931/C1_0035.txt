Wednesday — April 6-/27 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday April 6-/27 — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] — Present Mmes — Dick Palache Hoffmann Stone Holden Wilson Tufts Leavitt Matson Rossier [?] Griffith Frink & McWilliams. — Secty's report was read & accepted — Supt's & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed — Treasurer's report also read — Bal. on hand Feb. 28 — $17334.98 Disbursements for the month $7267.42 Bal on hand March 31-/27 $13987.65 [?]

Applications —

Mr. Henry Rohlfs [?] age 75 — came before the Board to speak for himself & wife — he was accepted as he had his report from Dr. Rummell [?] — his wifes acceptance is pending until we have her certificate from her doctor — on motion made by Mrs. Matson & sec. by Mrs. Griffith —

Miss Josephine Jacobsen came before the Board again as last month she did not have the doctors report — as it was satisfactory she was accepted — on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Frink — & to be notified when we have a vacancy —

Mrs. Eleanor J. Pierce age 79 — she wishes to pay $3000 & $150.00 — & was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Griffith & placed on the waiting list —

Simon [?] Peter Fraser age 74 — wished to pay $3150.00 or $3650 & was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt — as we have no room at present he will be notified.

The President of the Board named the nominating Committee & appointed Mrs. Haven Matson & Frink —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday May 4 [?] /27 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
