Wednesday [struck through: Sept] Oct [?] 6-/26 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday Sept. 6-/26 — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] — Present Mmes Dick Pritchard Tufts Holden Bates Griffith Matson Leavitt Andrews Palache Rossier [?] Haven Frink McWilliams — Sectys report was read & accepted Supt's & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed — Treasurers report was read — Bal. on hand Aug 31-/26 $15138.36 Disbursements for the month $6561.78 [?] — Bal on hand Sept 30 /26 — $9508.63 [?] on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec by Mrs Andrews it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing committee

Mr. E. N. Teel [?] also Miss Helen McLean have finished their probationary time & on motion made by Mrs Bates & sec. by Mrs. Griffith they be admitted as regular members —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Andrews the purchasing of the coffee & tea urns & a motor for the ice cream freezer be looked into & investigated by the dining room Committee

On motion made by Mrs. Bates & sec. by Mrs Andrews the suggestion given by Dr. Morrill [?] to pro rate or a certain percentage allowed any one not satisfied with living in the Home be taken into consideration —

Applications

Mike Lapuh [?] age 66 — on motion made by Mrs Frink & sec. by Mrs. Griffith he was declined as there was no room at present & to look else where — he wishes to pay $3000.00 & 150.00

Mary J. Murphy age 65 — her application was read last meeting but as she did not appear she was not discussed — on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Frink she was accepted & put on the waiting list but should it be after six months she must have another report from the doctor she wishes to pay $3500 & 150.00

Agnes Augusta Pitman [?] age 82 — was not accepted as the Board did not feel she would be contented here — she wishes to pay $4150.00

Mrs Kate McCreadey [?] age 72 — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Haven & sec. by Mrs Matson & put on the waiting list, she wishes to pay $3000.00 & 150.00

Mrs Frink proposed having Mrs. Hurd [?] come before the Board for complaints she made against the food —

Mrs Palache suggested that Mrs. Dick our Pres. have a talk with Mrs Stamp [?] freeing her from all blame of the complaints about the food —

On motion made by Mrs. Matson & sec. by Mrs Frink that if the De Breitreeles [?] change their room for the two vacated by the death of Miss Wilson, they will have to pay 100.00 for the cleaning [continues on next page]
