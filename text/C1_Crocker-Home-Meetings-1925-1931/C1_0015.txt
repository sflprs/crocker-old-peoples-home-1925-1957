Wednesday Jan 6 —/26 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday Jan 6 /26 at 10.30 A.M. —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] —

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Pritchard, Tufts Wilson Leavitt Griffith Hoffmann Palache Judson Bates Haven Petersen Plume [?] Matson Andrews Holden Frink [?] & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read accepted & filed —

Supt's & Matrons reports also read & accepted —

Treasurers report was read — Bal. on hand Nov 20099.00 Disbursements for the month $8453.19 — Bal. on hand Dec. 31 — $16524.12 in the Crocker Bank — On motion made by Mrs. Matson & sec. by Mrs. Andrews it is to be referred to the auditing committee

The new lavatories on the second floor are to be left in the hands of the President on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Tufts —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Plume [?] all the loud speaking of the radios are to be discontinued at 9 o'clock in the evening in the members rooms — ear phones may be used after that hour —

The renewing [?] of the garden will be looked into by Mrs. Hodgen who knows of a good gardener —

Applications — Mr & Mrs Lacey [?] accepted as regular members Bates & Tufts —

George Nichol [?] Gordon Halliday age 68 — he wishes to pay 3500 & 150.00 but having but of $8000.00 will have to pay $2500.00 not having a room at present have put him on the waiting list — he was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Plume [?] & sec. by Mrs. Griffith —

Mrs Catherine M. [?] Estey [?] age 65 — she wishes to pay $2500.00 & 150.00 did not appear at the Home on Board day —

Mrs. Bertha Ryburn [?] age 65 — on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs Griffith she was not accepted owing to ill health & no room available — the Committee was authorized to attend to her case.

Mrs Eleanor Lyell [?] Abadie [?] who was accepted into the Home as a working member was called before the Board to be told just how much sewing she must do — it was decided that she must sew 12 scarfs & 3 pairs of curtains a week & catch up with the needed supply & also to do any other sewing Mrs Stump [?] may have for her to do on motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Bates —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Feb 3 — /26 —

                        Respectfully submitted
