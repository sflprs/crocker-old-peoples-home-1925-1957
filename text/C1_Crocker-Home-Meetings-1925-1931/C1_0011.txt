The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday Oct 7-/25 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?].

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Palache Pritchard Hoffmann Griffith Judson Tufts Holden Rossier [?] Matson Andrews Bates Leavitt Plume [?] Frink [?]

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed —

Treasurers report was read & accepted on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Plume [?] & to be referred to the auditing committee Bal. on hand $16906.94 Disbursements for the month $6920.46 Bal on hand end of month $18512.62

Applications

James C. Perkins age 74 — wishes to pay $2650.00 & $150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Bates & sec. by Mrs. Griffith he was accepted & put on the waiting list —

Miss Frances Johnston age 65 — wishes to pay $3000.00 & 150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs Bates she was accepted — also put on the waiting list.

Benjamin F. Ritter [?] age 70 — also wishes to pay 3000.00 & 150.00 as we have no rooms he was also put on the waiting list & to be put on the table —

Mrs Katherine Thompson age 72 — wishes to pay 3000.00 & 150.00 also to be put on the table — until a vacant room is available. on motion made by Mrs. Bates & sec. by Mrs. Plume [?] —

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Tufts the application committee is to make their investigations with the applicants & they are to come to the next Board meeting

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Petersen that the Board give the rooms committee the power to demand the rooms from Mr. Smyth & on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Frink [?] that he be assigned to room 279 —

Mention was made that the buying committee is to purchase the necessary linen & to take advantage of the present prices —

On motion made by Mrs Frink [?] & sec. by Mrs Hodgen the hospital committee is to purchase & use their own discretion as to the buying of hospital beds —

A letter is to be written to the Secty of the Napa Asylum — thanking for the attention & care given Mrs. Smyth — on motion made by Mrs. Frink [?] & sec. by Mrs Petersen —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Nov. 5 /25

                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
