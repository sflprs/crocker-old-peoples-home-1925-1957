Wednesday April 3-/29-

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday April 3-/29 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Rev. Dunham —

Present Mmes - Dick Hodgen [?] Andrews Wilson Stephenson Paddock Tufts Matson Plume [?] Wilcox Borda Haven

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supts Matrons & Head nurses reports also read accepted & filed

Treasurers report read - Bal on hand Feb 28/29 - $52022.65 [?] Disbursements for the month $13419.13 Bal on hand March 31-/29 - checking acct $15950.25 Savings Acct $27759.29 Total $43472.54 [?] Recd from the Wm [?] G. Lane [?] Estate cash $1285.14 Bonds $1000.00 Total $2285.14 On motion made by Mrs Haven & sec by Mrs Borda it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

Miss Ready & Miss Sykes will finish their probation April 15. on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Matson they are to be accepted as regular members when they have completed their probation —

On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Borda a letter is to be written to the daughter of Mrs. Ross, that a nurse must be provided for her either in the Home or to be cared for on the outside

Applications —

Mr. H. M. Britton [?] age 69 — he wishes to pay 3000 & 150.00 — & also will pay the 1000.00 for extra care & to receive 4% on that amount on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Matson he was accepted. a friend G. H. Evans [?] will provide for him - funeral expenses will also be provided —

Mrs. Mary Bassett [?] Hoet [?] age 84 — she wishes to pay 3000 & 150.00 probation, & 1000 for extra care at 4% Int. she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Wilcox & sec. by Mrs. Tufts

Wm Raynes [?] age 65 — his application was not considered as he did not appear —

Mrs. Dick named the nominating committee - Mrs. Andrews Chairman - Mrs. Borda & Mrs. Tufts.

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday May 1-/29 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
