Wednesday May 1-/29 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Old Peoples Home was held by the Lady Board of Managers Wednesday May 1-/29-

The meeting was opened with prayer by Rev. Dunham —

Present Mmes Dick Hodgen [?] Wilson Matson Borda Wilcox Tufts Andrews Leavitt [struck through: Borda] Paddock, Holden Plume [?] - McWilliams

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's Matrons & Head nurses reports also read, accepted & filed —

Treasurers report was read - Bal on hand March 31-/29 - $43472.54 Disbursements for the month 9918.26 Bal April 30/29 Checking Acct 15893.69 Savings Acct 29748.79 Totals in both accts 45840.58 [?] - 45572.48 [?] actual Bal.

Emergency Acct 5019.80 Hospital 5995.14 Special Care fund 18463.00 Int. $270.85

On motion made by Mrs Borda & sec. by Mrs Holden it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

Applications —

Mrs Louise Wilcox age 65 - wishes to pay 3150 - as she did not appear her case was not discussed —

On motion made by Mrs. Matson & sec by Mrs. Wilcox Mrs Schadler's [?] resignation as Head Nurse be accepted & she is to receive two weeks pay in lieu of her vacation —

On motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Plume [?] the present situation regarding the nurses be left to Mrs. Dick to decide.

On motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Tufts the night watchman is to be let out —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday June 5-/29 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
