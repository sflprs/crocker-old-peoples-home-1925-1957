Wednesday Feb 3 — /26 — [heading partly cut off at the top of the photo]

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Feb 3 — /26 —

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Pritchard Andrews Hoffmann Griffith Bates Judson Holden Tufts Matson Haven Wilcox Leavitt Palache Frink [?] & McWilliams —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed —

Treasurers report was read Bal on hand $16524.12 Disbursements for the month $7312.16 Bal on hand Jan 31 — 20689.88 — on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Matson it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

Applications —

Mr. F. [?] Pickering [?] & Mr & Mrs. Geo Walters have finished their probation time & on motion made by Mrs Bates & sec. by Mrs. Griffith they were accepted as regular members

Mr Jas. W. Spencer [?] age 66 — was not considered owing to ill health —

Mrs Ella Corbett [?] Murphey [?] age 80 — she wishes to pay 2500.00 & the 150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Wilcox she was accepted —

Mrs Elizabeth Leishman [?] Moore age 68 — also wishes to pay $2500 & 150 — on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Tufts she was accepted —

Mr. George Booth [?] age 66 — wishes to pay $2500 & 150 on motion made by Mrs. Griffith & sec. by Mrs. Wilcox he was accepted — but as he has gout [?] he must pay $400.00 extra —

Mrs. Catherine A. Estey [?] age 65 — on motion made by Mrs. Holden & sec. by Mrs. Bates she was accepted she wishes to pay $2500 & 150.00

It was decided that the hospital proposition be left to the committee

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday March 3 /26 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        A. M. McWilliams —
