Wednesday June 4-/30 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Lady Board of Managers was held Wednesday June 4-/30 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham —

Present Mmes - Leavitt Paddock Guy Matson Wheeler [?] Palache Holden Stevenson Haven Andrews Bailhache [?] & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read accepted & filed —

Supt's Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read & accepted on motion made by Mrs. Holden & sec. by Mrs Matson —

On motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Stevenson the past 2 meetings be accepted as read - one being a Special meeting —

On motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Stevenson the election of Mrs Borda at the last meeting as Ass't Secty & Treasurer be rescinded naming Mrs Holden for those positions —

Treasurers report was read - Bal on hand April 30/30 $2667.64 Disbursements for the month Bal on hand May 31-/30 - [struck through: 2975] $2975.44 The auditing committee report for last month was found to be correct. On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs Matson to be accepted & referred to the auditing Committee —

A letter is to be written to Mrs Emma A. Matignon [?] explaining the 1000.00 Receipt has regarding a loan be corrected for her & kindly explain her arrangement with Mrs. Dick regarding the payment of the $1000.00

On motion made by Mrs Matson & sec. by Mrs Paddock Mrs Poggon [?] is to be termed office Secty with duties suitable for this position.

The executive committee is to meet Mr. Kelly who took Mrs. Searle [?] away from the Home on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Haven, the situation to be left to the President & Executive Committee —

Mr Kelly came before the Board & explained to the Board that Mrs Searle [?] wishes to be taken to a Hospital — where she will remain for a few days

A note to be written Dr. Dunham telling him of Mrs Poggon [?] new name of office Secty.

There being no further business the meeting stands adjourned,

                        Respectfully submitted,
                        A. M. McWilliams —
                             Secty —
