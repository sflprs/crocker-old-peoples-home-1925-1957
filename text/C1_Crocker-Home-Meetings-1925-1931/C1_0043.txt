Wednesday Oct 5-/27 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday Oct 5-/27 — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?]. Present Mmes — Dick Hodgen Hoffmann Griffith Wilson Borda Holden Palache Haven Leavitt Tufts Andrews Wilcox Rossier [?] Frink & McWilliams — Secty's report was read & accepted — Supt & Matron's reports also read accepted & filed — Treasurers report was read — Bal on hand Sept 1st $14345.21 [?] Disbursements for the month 7304.82 Bal on hand Sept 30-/27 — $16861.64 [?]

On motion made by Mrs. Leavitt & sec. by Mrs Griffith the Secty is to notify Dr Morrill [?] the Supt. that in the case of Mr. Temple there can be no reconsideration —

Applications —

Nellie C. Lane [?] age 74 — who came in between meetings was accepted by the Board on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Frink — she paid $5150.00

Frank Victor [?] Jenkins [?] age 65 — wishes to pay 3150.00 he was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Frink & sec. by Mrs. Andrews —

Mrs. A. McTigue [?] age 67 — also wishes to pay 3150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Borda & sec. by Mrs Rossier [?] she was rejected as she is able to support herself on the outside

Miss Martha Murphy age 65 — also wishes to pay 3150.00 as Dr. Rummell [?] rejected her on account of her physical condition on motion made by Mrs. Rossier [?] & sec. by Mrs Holden —

Mrs. Julia Vollmer [?] age 64+ & wishes to pay 4150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Griffith she was accepted & to be admitted when of age —

Mrs Jennie L. Katzenstein age 64+ she also wishes to pay $4150.00, accepted on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Wilcox, also to be admitted when of age

Mrs. Ida Field [?] age 73 — $3150.00 she wishes to pay she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Griffith & sec by Mrs. Borda —

Mrs. Bickford who came into the Home in August is not satisfied & has requested her money returned to her — the amount is 4000.00

Mrs George Bates has sent in her resignation as a member of the Board of Managers, the Secty is to write her asking to reconsider her decision & to wait a few months when she might feel differently about it —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Hodgen 500.00 of the bill for Mrs Lang's [?] funeral & expenses be paid by the House, Mr Lang [?] offered to pay the balance —

On motion by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Tufts in the future, Mr. Lang [?] be paid int. on 1/2 of the Div. checks on shares of P. G. [?] — [continues on next page; last line partly cut off at the bottom of the photo: "be given Mr. Lang [?] —"]
