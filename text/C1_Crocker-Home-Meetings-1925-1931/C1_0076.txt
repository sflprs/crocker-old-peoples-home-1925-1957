The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Thursday Jan 2-/30 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham —

Present Mmes - Leavitt who presided in Mrs. Dick's absence - Hodgen [?] Wilson, Stephensen Holden Borda Matson Palache Tufts Andrews & McWilliams & Walkup [?] —

Secty's report was read & accepted

Supt's Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read accepted & filed —

Treasurers report was read - Bal. on hand Nov 30-/29 — $25241.11 Disbursements for the month 7842.26 [?] Bal on hand to our acct in bank $4324.87 [?] Dec 31-/29

Application

Mrs. C. G. Parker room 332 will complete her probation Jan 19 - on motion made by Mrs. Tufts & sec. by Mrs. Holden she is to be accepted as a regular member

Mr. Richard Powell [?] room 260 - he was accepted & the action of the advisory board be ratified - on motion made by Mrs Tufts & sec. by Mrs. Borda - he paid 4000 & 150.00

Herman Anderson - age 70 - on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Holden he was admitted - wishes to pay 4000 & 150.00

Mrs. Augusta Triebel [?] age 78 - she applied in 1924 - but made out a new application blank, and statement of finances - pending doctors certificate. she will be admitted on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Walkup [?]. she decided on room 203 —

Mrs Mary V. Cunningham age 66 - wishes to pay 4500 & 150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Tufts & sec. by Mrs Matson she was accepted. Room 401 —

The hospital Committee is to take charge of Mrs Ross case to decide - on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Tufts —

On motion made by Mrs Walkup [?] & sec. by Mrs Stephensen all donations at Xmas time to receive notes of thanks —

Dr. Dunham is to communicate with each of the heads of other departments in receipt of any complaints to be referred to him —

On motion made by Mrs Borda & sec. by Mrs Wilson the dental work of Mrs Searle [?] to be put into the hands of Miss Burr [?] the head nurse

The buying Committee is to purchase the required linen for the kitchen & hospital department on motion made made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Holden —

On motion made by Mrs Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs. Andrews the complaint of the coffee & tea to be referred to the dining room Committee.
