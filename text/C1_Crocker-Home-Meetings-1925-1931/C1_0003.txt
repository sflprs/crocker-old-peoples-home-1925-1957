Wednesday June 3-/25 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday June 3-1925 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Mr. Chambers — Present Mmes. Dick Palache Pritchard Griffith Andrews Wilcox Holden Hodgen Borda [?] Leavitt Haven [?] Frink [?] Petersen Matson & McWilliams

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Treasurers report also read Bal on hand $20223.41 Disbursements for the month $7051.32 — Bal at end of month $15296.19 — on motion made by Mrs Borda [?] & seconded by Mrs Holden it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

Supt's & Matrons reports read accepted & filed on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Frink [?] —

On motion made by Mrs. Petersen & sec. by Mrs Holden the resignations of Mr & Mrs. Chambers be accepted & placed on file —

Applications

Jacob L. Baker age 67 wishes to pay $2500 plus 150 he was accepted on motion made by Mrs Haven [?] & sec. by Mrs. Wilcox.

Wm Alonzo Burton age 69 — wishes to pay $3000.00 & 150.00 on motion made by Mrs Haven [?] & sec. by Mrs. Wilcox he was accepted —

Anna G. Parmace [?] age 64 — she wished to pay 2650.00 & 150.00 but as she was under age was not considered

Martha McIntyre on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Holden she was placed on the waiting list —

Mrs Mary Meyer age 71 — she wished to pay $2500 & 150 but on motion made by Mrs Matson & sec. by Mrs. Frink [?] she was not accepted —

Henry Schroeder age 65 — would pay 2500 plus 150 — on motion by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs Borda [?] was placed on the waiting list

Mrs Isabella Johnson age 68 — wished to pay 3000 & 150 on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Andrews was also placed on the waiting list

Milton Kelly age 76 wished to pay 2500 & 150

Mrs. Jennie Giacomini —

A letter was read from Mrs Jones thanking the managers for the 200.00

On motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Matson the original proposition about the furniture of Mrs. Hull is to be adhered to, of 3 pieces & to designate which two pieces they desire to take —
