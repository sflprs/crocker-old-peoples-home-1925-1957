Wednesday Dec 1-/26 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday Dec 1 /26 — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?]. Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Pritchard Stone Hoffmann [?] Griffith Wilcox Wilson Borda Blum [?] Tufts Matson [struck through: Holden] Bates Holden Andrews Haven Petersen Frink & McWilliams Secty's report was read & accepted — Supt's & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed — Treasurers report also was read — Bal on hand Oct. 31 — $15654.42 Disbursements for the month $7511.91 Bal on hand Nov. 30 — $9586.38 [?] [figure partly overwritten] The reports from the auditing committee for the months August Sept & Oct. were handed in & found correct —

The carpets left [?] at Spauldings & regarding the moths in the carpets were discussed & on motion made by Mrs. Blum [?] & sec. by Mrs. Matson — the new carpet at Spauldings be brought to the Home & kept here until used for safe keeping —

Applications — Mrs. Olive Wheeler [?] & Mrs Bertha [?] Jaume [?] have finished their probation — on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs Wilson they were accepted as regular members of the Home.

Josephine Burmeister age 71 — called again having been here last month — she wishes to pay 4000.00 & 150. — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Wilcox & sec. by Mrs Haven —

Mrs T. Van Drake age 65 — wishes to pay $4000 & 150. — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Hoffmann [?] & sec. by Mrs. Andrews —

Mrs Eugenia Morrison age 64 — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Matson — she will pay $3500 & 150 —

Mrs Elizabeth Black age 70 — was at the Sept meeting — wishes to pay $3000 & 150.00 she was not considered & thought unsatisfactory —

Miss Fannie Burke — was also at the last meeting but did not have the Dr. certificate — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Frink & sec. by Mrs Petersen —

D. W. Bales [?] — 72 wishes to pay $3600 [?] & 150.00 but did not appear

Xmas festivities were discussed — Mrs Griffith will attend to the candy — Mrs Judson very kindly will donate the tree & most acceptable — It was decided we would purchase bon bons for each member — More berries are to be bought & less garlands — A gift for each member is also to be purchased by the Committee.

A letter is to be written Mr. Murphy asking to have the committee that was appointed at the annual meeting to have a meeting to arrange our contracts — on motion made by Mrs Leavitt & sec. by Mrs Blum [?]

On motion made by Mrs. Frink & sec. by Mrs Tufts to give the Supt the authority to keep the organ locked & to use his discretion in giving permission to use it

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Jan 5/27 —
