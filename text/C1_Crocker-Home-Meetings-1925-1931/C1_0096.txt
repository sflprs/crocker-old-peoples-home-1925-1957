Wednesday March 4/31 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday March 4-/31 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham [?] —

Present Mmes. Leavitt Paddock Dick Wilson Tufts Wilcox Palache [?] Bates [?] Andrews Borda [?] Stevenson Gay Matson Haven [?] Hodgen [?] & McWilliams.

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt. Matrons & Head Nurses report also read accepted & filed

Treasurers report also read - Bal. on hand Jan 31-/31 - $1975.54 [?] Disbursements for the month 7431.86 Bal. on hand Feb. 28/31 $2043.68 on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Matson it was accepted on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs Matson & to be referred to the auditing committee —

Mrs Sylvia Bishop has finished her probation & on motion made by Mrs. Tufts & sec. by Mrs Borda [?] she was accepted for membership in the Home

Applications —

Mrs Fahey [?] came before the Board - her application was read at the last meeting & she wishes to pay $4150.00 on motion made by Mrs Paddock & sec by Mrs Gay she was accepted as a probationary member of the Home —

Miss Marie Mackenzie age 69 — she wishes to pay $4150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs Borda [?] she was accepted —

Mrs. Anna [?] Ford Reel [?] age 65 - she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Palache [?] & sec by Mrs Matson - price will be determined later —

Mrs. Louise [?] H. Betts [?] who applied in May/28 - came before the Board to make a new application for membership but could not be accepted owing to lack of funds —

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs Tufts a nominal sum to be asked Miss Reedy for the furniture in Mrs Sykes [?] room —

On motion made by Mrs Palache [?] & sec. by Mrs. Andrews if Mrs Powell decides on a smaller room, no rebate will be allowed her on her present larger room, but will be given a new carpet - she will also have the privilege of changing back if dissatisfied by paying the cleaning of the rooms

On motion made by Mrs. Wilcox & sec. by Mrs Hodgen [?] it was sanctioned [?] changing the seating in the dining room & to be referred to the dining room & kitchen committee

It was decided to paper the dining room on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs Paddock —

On motion made by Mrs Wilcox & sec. by Mrs Matson, the heads of the departments are each to take a week end vacation each month granted by the Board - the arrangement of the help was to be in the hands of the Supt.

Miss Poggon [?] also to be included
