Wednesday Nov 6-/29 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Nov 6-/29 —

Present Mmes - Dick Hodgen [?] Stevenson Paddock Leavitt Tufts Matson Haven Wilson & McWilliams

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham —

Secty's report was read accepted & filed —

Supt's, Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read & accepted

Treasurers report was read Bal. on hand Sept 30-/29 — $20862.39 Disbursements for the month $7628.86 Bal. on hand Oct 31-/29 - 25173.53 [?]

On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt all money due Mrs Stevens who withdrew from the Home owing to family objections be returned to her. She only remained in the Home two weeks.

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs. Matson the minimum price for membership in the Old Peoples Home is to be 4000.00 plus 150.00 probation - Secretary to write Mr. Buckley [?] of the Board of Trustees to that effect. —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Dec. /29 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
