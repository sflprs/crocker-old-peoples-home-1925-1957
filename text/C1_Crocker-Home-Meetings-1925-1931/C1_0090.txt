Wednesday Dec 3-/30 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Dec 3-/30 - The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham [?]

Present Mmes. - Leavitt Paddock [?] Wilson Gay Dick Wilcox Matson Stevenson Bailhache [?] Palache [?] Haven [?] Bates [?] Andrews Hodgen [?] Maillard [?] & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's Matrons & Head nurses also read accepted & filed —

Treasurer's report also read — Bal on hand Oct 31/30 - $1714.56 Disbursements for the month 6994.98 Bal on hand Nov 30/30 $2219.58 on motion made by Mrs Matson & sec by Mrs Wilson it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

On motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs Hodgen [?] that Carl Wellans [?] Grouts [?] who has been in the employ of the Home for the past twenty three years, be offered a permanent home & to become a regular member of the Old Peoples Home in consideration & appreciation for his long & faithful services on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec by Mrs Wilcox he is to occupy Mr Smyth's room but should Mr Smyth return he would be assigned another room —

On motion made by Mrs Haven [?] & sec. by Mrs Gay a room is to be fitted up for the visiting nurses & selection of the room to be left to the discretion of the hospital & room Committees —

Mrs Whicher [?] was appointed chairman of the room Committee in Mrs Palache's [?] place —

On motion made by Mrs Stevenson & sec by Mrs Palache [?] a committee is to be formed comprising the President & two other members of the Board to confer with two trustees to discuss the plans & means toward a new building

On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs Gay an assistant to Mr Boock [?] to care for the gardens be employed.

Applications

Miss Julia McGee age 67 - she wishes to pay $4000.00 & 150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs Haven [?] she was accepted —

[struck through: On motion]

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Jan 7-/31 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams Secty

Moved - That the President appoint a Comm. of three consisting of herself & two other members of this Board. that she communicate with the Board of Directors & request the appointment of a Committee of three from that body. these six to constitute a Committee to consider ways & means for securing a new building & to report at the next annual meeting of the Corporation —
