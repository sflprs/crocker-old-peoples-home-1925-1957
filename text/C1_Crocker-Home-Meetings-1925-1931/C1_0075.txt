Wednesday Dec. 4-/29 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Dec 4-/29 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Mr. Dunham —

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen [?] Tufts Palache, Wilson Holden Stephensen - Paddock Haven Leavitt McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supts Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read & accepted —

Treasurers report was read - Bal on hand Oct 31-/29- $25173.53 Disbursements for the month $13909.23 but $1775 of that amount was a refund to Mrs Stevens who withdrew from the Home — Bal on hand Nov 30-/29- 25241.11

Mrs Virginia [?] Post has finished her probation Dec 11-/29 on motion made by Mrs Tufts & sec. by Mrs Haven she was accepted as a regular member. —

Applications

Mrs Frances A. Wheeler [?] age 73- she wishes to pay 4000 & 150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Paddock —

Mrs. Louise Wilcox age 65- has only 3000 — not having enough funds could not be accepted —

Mrs Hilda [?] Rummel [?] age 67- wishes to pay $4000 & 150.00

Mr. Nels C. Rummel [?] age 75 also wishes to pay $4000 & 150.00

they were [struck through: both] accepted on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Haven — on the old rate, & receive interest on their 2000.00 — they were accepted —

On motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs Holden - Mr Hall [?] & Mrs Sutcliffe [?] are to receive an extra 25.00 besides their $20.00 Xmas bonus for extra work they had done.

The gas jets [?] now in the toilets are to be removed & to be left [struck through: to the] discretion of the advisory board on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Tufts —

On motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Holden, that Mrs Hodgen [?] be appointed to attend to Mrs Searle [?] to acquire new teeth & the expense to be taken out of her Trust [?].

The Hospital & oculist, bills to be paid of Mrs. Manning on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs Wilson.

On motion made by Mrs Holden & sec. by Mrs Palache when Mrs Hodgen [?] the Treasurer requires more funds than the 7500 allowed for monthly expenses she is to confer with the advisory board who then with the finance committee for the necessary amount to cover the expenses —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Jan. /29 [?] —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams Secty —

The first Wednesday in January /30- falls on Jan 1-/30 therefore the meeting for Jan. was held Jan 2-/30 —
