Wednesday Jan 5-/27 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wed. Jan 5-/27 — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Pritchard Hoffmann [?] Griffith Palache Matson Tufts Borda Leavitt Bates Wilson Rossier [?] Wilcox Haven Stone Frink Petersen Blum [?] & McWilliams — Secty's report was read & accepted — Supt's & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed — Treasurers report was read — Bal on hand Nov 30 /26 — $9586.38 Disbursements for the month $7720.05 [?] Bal on hand Dec 31-/26. $7009.59 [?]

Mrs Anna Cecil & Mr. J. W. Pertlecleke [?] have finished their probation on motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec by Mrs. Hoffmann they were accepted as regular members —

Applications —

Helen Emeline [?] Healy age 75 — she wishes to pay $3000 & $150 she did not come to the meeting —

Mrs L. M. Treadwell [?] age 70 — she also wishes to pay $3000 & 150.00 the Dr's certificate was most unsatisfactory & troubled with cataracts — The Board decided she was ineligible for the Home.

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt — the hospital committee & the officers of the Board are to meet & frame the duties & responsibilities of the three heads of the departments —

On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Hoffmann a new presser for the laundry is to be purchased —

Mr Booth who takes care of the garden was discussed — as he was only being given 5.00 it was thought to do more for him — on motion made by Mrs. McWilliams & sec. by Mrs. Bates a check of 25.00 was to be given him — on talking it over it was to be considered later & to lie on the table for the present —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Feb. /27 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams —
