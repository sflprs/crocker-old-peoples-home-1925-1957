Special meeting /—
Wed June 22-/27 —

A Special meeting was called for 11 o'clock June 22-/27 Present Mmes. Dick Griffith Judson Frink Holden Andrews & McWilliams — as proxies were Mrs Haven & Hoffmann A motion was made to have Mrs. Jennie Petersen returned from Agnews where she has been confined for 21 months —

A letter was to be written to Mr. Hanlon [?] to make all necessary arrangements & asking him to communicate with the Supt. of Agnews & would he take all steps to have her returned & in what way to be returned — the ladies feel in having Mr Hanlon [?] attend to these proceedings he could do so in a legal way thereby protecting the Home from any further troubles — & if necessary to be sent back, it can be done

On motion made by Mrs. Frink & sec. by Mrs. Griffith these conditions were accepted —

Mrs Ludwig [?] one of the members of the Home complained that the Epsom Salts she has been given one morning by Mrs Schrader [?] the head nurse & insisted [?] carbolic acid was mixed in the salts. She was sent for & it was explained to her that the salts has been analyzed by the pharmacist & no trace of carbolic acid could be found — Dr. Morrill [?] & Mrs Schrader [?] were also called & in their presence convinced Mrs Ludwig [?] & exonerated Mrs. Schrader [?] from all blame — As this question was being discussed by various ones in the Home Dr Morrill [?] was requested to read the druggist's report in the dining room, so as to quiet all those who had heard of it — This motion was made by Mrs. Griffith & sec. by Mrs. Andrews —

There being no further business, the meeting adjourned

                        Respectfully submitted,
                        A. M. McWilliams —
