Wednesday May 4-/27 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday May 4-/27 — at the Old Peoples Home — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] — Present. Mmes. Dick, Palache, Griffith, Hoffmann, Bates Matson Wilcox Haven Blum [?] Leavitt Borda Wilson Tufts Petersen Rossier [?] Frink & McWilliams Secty's report was read & accepted — Matrons & Supt's reports also read accepted & filed — Treasurers report was read, Bal on hand March 31-/27 — $13987.65 Disbursements for the month 10737.59 [?] — Bal. on hand April 30. /27 $7857.84 [?] —

Applications —

Mrs Agnes McCreery [?] will finish her probation May 16 — on motion made by Mrs Tufts & sec. by Mrs Griffith she is to be accepted as a regular member —

Christina [?] Hansen came before the Board again — she was in the Home in 1916 for two months & left — came again a few years later & again left — on motion made by Mrs Matson & sec. by Mrs Hoffmann she is not to be readmitted —

Jennie Pauline Willis applied in Nov. /23 — & will return next meeting to tell us of her decision —

Miss Olive [?] Cook — age 71 — wishes to pay $3000 & 150.00 she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Rossier [?] & sec. by Mrs Tufts she was not satisfied with the room she looked at & will wait —

Mrs Julia [?] Levinson [?] age 69 — she wishes to pay between 3 & 4000.00 but will wait until she can secure a sunny room —

[blank space in original] Johnson applied again & wished to pay the 3600.00 to secure her room so it would be ready for her whenever she was ready to come, the final decision was that she would wait until the time she was ready —

Angeline Metzger [?] Higgins [?] age 73 she wishes to pay $3000 & 150.00 she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Griffith —

Oscar N. Elingdeyst [?] age 64 — as he was not of age & not satisfactory he was not accepted —

Mrs. E. L. Mills age 81 — she wishes to pay 3000 & 150 she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Griffith & sec. by Mrs. Matson —

On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Blum [?] all changes made in the nursing department be left to the chairman of the Hospital [struck through: department] Committee —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday. June 1-/27 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams —
