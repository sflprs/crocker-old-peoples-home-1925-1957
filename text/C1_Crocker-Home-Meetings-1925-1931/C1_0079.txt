Wednesday April 2-/30 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday April 2-/30 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham —

Present. Dick Leavitt Matson Wilson Hodgen [?] Stevensen Holden Palache Tufts Andrews & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supts. Matrons & Head nurses reports also read accepted & filed

Treasurers report was read Bal. on hand Feb 28-/30- $2442.93 Disbursements for the month $7487.03 Bal. on hand March 31-/30. $2455.70 The auditing Committee found the reports of Jan & Feb /30 to be correct — On motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Palache the report was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs Holden the resignation of Mrs. Rossier [?] as a member of the Board be accepted with deep regret - her contribution of 25.00 annually was also accepted —

Applications

Mrs L. Vercoutre [?] will finish her probation this month on motion made by Mrs Holden & sec. by Mrs. Matson she was accepted as a regular member of the Home —

Mrs Nell P. Kiedaisch [?] age 71 — she wishes to pay $4500 & $150.00 probation, on motion made by Mrs Palache, & sec. by Mrs Tufts she was accepted subject to the doctors certificate - she must also sign a paper that should she dispose of property she values at $10000 or after her death the Home can claim the proper percentage on value —

On motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Tufts Mrs Ward Maillard daughter of Mrs L. E. Petersen [?] was accepted as a contributing member of the Home.

On motion made by Mrs. Matson & sec. by Mrs. Andrews she was also accepted as a member of the Board —

The names of Mrs Frank Sommers, Mrs Gloucester [?] Willis & Mrs Arthur Bailhache [?] were suggested as possible & desirable members for our Board - letters to be written inviting them to become members

A letter is to be written to Miss Dorothy Dix of the Chronicle in answer to a letter she had published in the paper regarding a home for Old People - on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs Wilson inform Miss Dix, the Board was interested in her article & telling her we have just such a home in S. F. & not endowed - if it were we could possibly build a new home —

Nominating Committee was appointed by Mrs. Dick — the following were named Mrs. Palache Tufts & Stevensen.

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday May 7/30 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
