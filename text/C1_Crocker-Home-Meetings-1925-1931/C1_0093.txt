[Book page 85]

Wednesday Jan 7-/31 -

Matson be appointed to investigate the price relative to the renting of the ball room at the Fairmont for the purpose of giving a card party for the benefit of the Home —

On motion made by Mrs Palache [?] & sec. by Mrs Hodgen [?] a letter to be drafted & mailed to the principal lawyers of this city & bay regions, that the Home is not endowed & to have the Home in their minds as to legacies & the needs of the Home - Trustees are to be consulted —

On motion made by Mrs Dick & sec. by Mrs Gay the Trustees are to be consulted regarding the drafting of such a letter —

A letter is also to be written to the Trustees telling them of a committee appointed to meet with them regarding the possibility of a new building — & that Mrs Leavitt would like to be present at their next meeting of Monday next —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Feb 4-/31 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
