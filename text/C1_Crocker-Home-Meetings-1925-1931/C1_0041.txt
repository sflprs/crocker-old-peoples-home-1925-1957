Wednesday Aug 3-/27 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Aug 3-/27 — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] — Present Mmes. Dick Borda Matson Tufts Palache Stone Bates Holden Griffith Frink Blum [?] Hoffmann Petersen McWilliams — The Secty's report was read & accepted — Supt's & Matrons reports also were read accepted & filed — Head nurse's report was read — Treasurers reports also read for June & July Bal. on hand May 31-/27 — 11216.38 Disbursements for the month [struck through: 7643.49] 8679.64 [?] Bal. on hand June 30-/27 — 7643.49 — Report for July — Bal on hand June 30-/27 — 7643.49 Disbursements for the month 6967.41 [?] Bal on hand July 31 — $4644.64 [?]

On motion made by Mrs. Tufts & sec. by Mrs. Palache that our desires be communicated to Dr. Morrill [?] in writing & all questions must be answered in his report the following month —

On motion made by Mrs. Matson & sec. by Mrs Petersen the Board is wishing for the Head Nurse to solve the problem in regards an extra nurse for the alley —

On motion made by Mrs. Petersen & sec. by Mrs. Blum [?] a vote for all confidence in our Pres. Mrs Dick, to handle Mr. Anderson's case as she sees fit —

Applications —

John Guinasso [?] age 73 — wishes to pay 3000 & 150.00 but did not appear —

Mrs A. M. Bickford [?] age 67 — wishes to pay 4000 & 150.00 she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Griffith & sec. by Mrs. Frink —

Lewis [?] P. Temple age 66 — wishes to pay 3000 & 150. — he was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Frink & sec. by Mrs. Blum [?] —

Hans Peter Jorrey [?] age 70 — wishes to pay between 3 or 4000 & 150.00 — also accepted on motion made by Mrs. Hoffmann & sec. by Mrs Tufts —

John McGuire [?] age 68 — on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Griffith he was accepted wishes to pay 3000 & 150 —

On motion made by Mrs Blum [?] & sec. by Mrs. Tufts the night nurse & the relief nurses are to have $5.00 added to their salary — making 70.00 per month —

Mr. Chas Hall [?] our engineer is to be married on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs Wilson the secty is to write a letter with all good wishes & a raise of 10.00 per month in his salary —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Sept. /27

                        Respectfully submitted
