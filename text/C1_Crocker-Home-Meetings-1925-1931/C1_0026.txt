Dick Hodgen Pritchard Hoffmann [?] Griffith Wilson Blum [?] Haven Wilcox Stone [?] Borda Holden Leavitt Tufts Rossier [?] Johnson [?] Frink & McWilliams The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr Morrill [?] — Secty's report was read & accepted — Supt's & Matrons report also read accepted & filed — Treasurers Report also read — Bal on hand July 31 — $13086.04 Disbursements [struck through: on hand] for the month 6469.04 [?] Bal on hand $15138.36 [?] Aug 31-/26 — on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Borda it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing committee —
