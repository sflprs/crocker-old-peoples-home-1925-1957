Wednesday Feb 5-/30 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday Feb 5-/30 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham —

Present Mmes. Dick Leavitt Hodgen [?] Haven Walkup [?] Andrews Wilson Tufts Holden Palache Stevenson Paddock Matson Borda & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's - Matrons & Head Nurses report were read accepted & filed

Treasurer's report also read - Bal. on hand $4324.87 Dec 31-/29 Disbursements for the month 8836.60 [?] Bal. on hand Jan 31/30 $2988.87 [?] — On motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Borda it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee

Applications —

Mr. Richard Powell [?] will finish his probation Feb 24-/30 — on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs Matson he was accepted as a regular member —

Mrs. Louise Vercoutre [?] age 65 - she wishes to pay & 150.00 & has selected room 256 - on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Borda she was accepted —

Mr Dunham was called before the Board to discuss the Stranberg [?] - it was then decided to have Mr Stranberg [?] come before the Board — he withdrew his application, as he will have enough to care for himself on the outside

On motion made by Mrs Matson & sec. by Mrs Stephenson the request of the Dr. Breitwieser [?] for the return of 250.00 be denied them & not allowed to give up one of their rooms they are to be notified - also Mr. Dunham —

On motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Holden Mr. Flaherty our Atty is to word the paragraph relating to medical care for members so as to protect the Home from imposition —

Mrs Dick made a suggestion whereon a motion was made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Tufts that Mrs Hodgen [?] Treasurer have Mr. Hickie [?] assist her when necessary & she be free to call upon him any time she feels it necessary to do so —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday March /30 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
