Wednesday Nov. 7 /28

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Nov 7 /28

Present Mmes Dick Hodgen Wilson Palache Tufts [?] Stephenson Wilcox Matson Leavitt Stone Haven Andrews, McWilliams

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham [?] our new Superintendent —

The Secretary's report was read & accepted —

The Matrons & Head Nurse's reports also read accepted & filed —

The Treasurer's report was read — Bal on hand Sept. 30/28 — 40091.88 Disbursements for the month 18959.44 [?] Bal on hand Oct 31 — 29214.00 [?] Savings Bank 19638.52 [?] Total balance 48852.52 [?] 5018.50 [?] Emergency Fund. The auditors report for last month was found to be correct —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Andrews an investigation is to be made through our attorney Mr. Flaherty [?] & to be try to get a rebate from Mrs Morrisons estate — according to our clause in the contract not declaring the full amount of their finances — Mrs Morrison claimed to be absolutely without funds — & was found after her death to have $8090.00 [?]

On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Matson Mrs H. L. [?] Paddock to be asked to become an associate member —

On motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Haven Mrs. Paddock was to be invited to become a member of the Board of Managers —

On motion made by Mrs. Leavitt & sec. by Mrs. Tufts [?] the committee, that was formed to consult with the Trustees regarding the revision of our contracts, to meet during the coming month, & to embody clauses desirable for our protection & to report to the Board at the next meeting —

On motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Tufts [?], Mrs Leavitt also to be added to that same committee —

Applications —

Mrs. Amanda L. E. Lorenz [?] age 64 + not having sufficient finances to come in & pay the extra $1000.00 for extra care & also wishing to wait several [?] years, preferred to wait —

John Squire Cunyer [?] age 72 — wished to pay 3000 & 150 the son Ralph Cunyer [?] will assume responsibility for special care & nursing on motion made by Mrs. Wilcox & sec. by Mrs. Andrews he was accepted —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Dec. 5-/28 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
