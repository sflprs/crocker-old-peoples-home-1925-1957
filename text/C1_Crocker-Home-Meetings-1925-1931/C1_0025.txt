Wednesday — Aug 4-/26 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home, was held Wednesday Aug 4-/26 Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Holden Borda Hoffmann [?] Griffith Wilson Palache Wilcox Andrews Blum [?] Bates Rossier [?] Frink & McWilliams — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] — The Secretary's report was read & accepted. Supt's & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed — Treasurers report was read — of the months of June & July — Bal on hand May 31 — $16066.70 Disbursements for the month $8708.08 [?] Bal on hand $8923.12 [?] June 30-/26 — Disbursements for July $6985.35 [?] Bal on hand July 31-/26. $13086.04 [?] On motion made by Mrs Bates & sec. by Mrs. Griffith the report is to be referred to the auditing Committee

On motion made by Mrs Blum [?] & sec. by Mrs Borda the piano in one of the upstairs sitting rooms is to be disposed of —

A motion was made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs Frink that Mr. Hall [?] the engineer is to be paid $65.00 toward his vacation that he should have received for last years vacation which he was unable to take as he was needed in the House & making it impossible for him to go. —

The probationary time of Mr. George Booth has expired on motion made by Mrs. Wilcox & sec. by Mrs Griffith he was accepted as a regular member of the House — he takes excellent care of the Garden —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday September 1st/26 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        A. M. McWilliams —
