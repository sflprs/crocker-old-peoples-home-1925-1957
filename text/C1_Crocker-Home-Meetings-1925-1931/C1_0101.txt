Annual Meeting
May. 20th 1931.

The annual meeting of the Board of Lady Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held in the Board room on May 20th 1931. at 3.45 p.m. Mrs Leavitt, The President, in the Chair

Those present were Mesdames: Dick, Walkup, Paddock, Tufts, Hodgen, Matson, Bates, Stevenson, Wilcox, Bailhache, Wicher, Wilson, and Borda.

The Nominating Committee presented the regular Ticket for the election of President and Officers for the Board of Lady Managers for the ensuing year. There being no further nominations offered, a vote was taken and was unanimously carried. The Secretary was requested to cast the Ballot, announcing the election of the following officers.

    President    Mrs J. W. Leavitt
    1st. Vice President    Mrs E. W. Wilson
    2nd. "  "    Mrs W. E. Wilcox
    3rd. "  "    Mrs J. B. Tufts.
    Secretary.    Mrs J. P. Borda -
    Treasurer.    Mrs E. M. Paddock.
    Assistant Secy & Treas.    Mrs R. V. Holden.

Mrs C. Crawford, night nurse, submitted a resume of her night duties. The question of her duties and salary was duly discussed. Mrs Wilson moved and Mrs Matson seconded that Mrs Crawford be kept on as Night Nurse at her present salary. - Motion carried.

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned -

                        Respectfully Submitted
                        Josephine P. Borda
                             Secy. pro tem
