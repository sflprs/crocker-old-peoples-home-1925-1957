Wednesday Nov. 2-/27 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Nov 2-/27 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?]

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Hoffmann Griffith Matson Borda Helfer [?] Andrews Haven Frink Leavitt Wilson & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read accepted & filed —

Supt & Matrons reports also read & accepted —

Treasurers report was read Bal on hand Sept. 30-/27-16861.64 Disbursements 12300.51 including the refund of 400.00 to Mrs. G. [?] M. Buck [?] fund [?] - Bal on hand Oct 31-/27 - 13402.35 [?] & was accepted & to be referred to the auditing committee on motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Andrews —

Regards Mrs Hegerty's [?] furniture it is to be placed in the hands of our Atty Mr. Flaherty [?] & a letter is to be written by the Secty - to Mrs. Coe [?] the niece who is claiming the furniture to call on our Atty at his earliest convenience -

The Supt is to be notified that no changes are to be made in the Contracts by the Supt or any one without the consent of the Board & Signature of the President on motion made by Mrs. Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Frink —

Dr Morrill [?] to be notified to return the letter received from Geo. [?] Stevenson [?] Atty for Miss J. Burnham [?] connected with the Eaton Estate to our Atty, to act upon it & also to copy a new bill of $175.00 against the Estate to be sent our Atty —

On motion made by Mrs Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Palache, the two Liberty Bonds left by Miss H. G. Lane [?] for Mrs Schroder [?] & Mr. Frederickson be given them

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt the stretchers asked for by Mrs Schroder [?] the nurse, be given her, 1 for each floor —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Andrews, Mrs. Griffith to have the purchasing of the candy for Xmas —

Applications —

John Pole [?] age 68 — wishes to pay 3000 + 150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Wilson he was accepted

Harlow [?] P. Livesgood [?] age 67 — wishes to pay 3000 + 150 he was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Wilson —

It was moved & seconded that a new carpet be purchased for the second floor hall — by Mrs. Andrews & Hoffmann —

Mrs. Dick appointed Mrs. Palache & Mrs. Andrews as a committee to secure bids for the building of a room & bath for the head nurse.

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday — Dec. 7th/27 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
