Wednesday June 5-/29 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Lady Board of Managers of the Old People Home was held Wednesday June 5-/29 —

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen [?] Stephenson Matson — Palache Wilson Haven Leavitt & McWilliams [struck through: Wilcox] —

Secty's report was read accepted & filed —

Supt's Matrons & Head Nurses reports were also read accepted & filed

Treasurer's report was read, Bal. on hand April 30-/29- $45572.48 Disbursements for the month 8043.60 Bal on hand, May 31- 38739.18 Checking Acct 8856.69 Savings acct $29882.49 on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs Leavitt it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

Applications —

Mrs May B. Hoet [?] & Mr. H. M. Britton [?] have completed their two months probation & on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Stephensen they were accepted as regular members

Mr. Shadrach [?] Harvey [?] age 65 — on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt he was not accepted owing to the unsatisfactory report from Dr. Rummell [?] regarding his health —

Mrs. Sarah Jane Phillips age 75 - she came before the Board but is not ready to come in until her property is sold - she has no cash on hand at present —

Mrs. Francis A. Atchison [?] 64 + - as she is not eligible she will return when she is 65 — she was not acted upon —

Sarah G. Burns age 75 - as she did not appear was not discussed —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt The Secty - is to act as Treasurer Pro Tem. in cases of emergency — & sign cards at the Bank to that effect —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt Mr. Dunham be requested to notify Mrs. Wilson one week before Board day the names of new members having finished their probation - as they might be interviewed before the Board meeting —

A new radio is to be installed on motion by Mrs Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs. Stephenson —

An advisory Board is to be appointed by Mrs. Dick to take care of any extra business that might come up between Board meetings —

On motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Stephenson in cases of emergency Mrs Hodgen [?] be given privilege to draw funds from the Trustees —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned [struck through: to] Wednesday August 7-/29 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
