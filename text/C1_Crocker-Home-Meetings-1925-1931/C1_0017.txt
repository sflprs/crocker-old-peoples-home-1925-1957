The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday March 3 — /26 —

Present Mmes. — Dick Hodgen Wilson Tufts Wilcox Hoffmann Griffith Palache Holden Bates Rossier [?] Stone Frink [?] Leavitt & McWilliams

The meeting was opened with prayer —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed —

Treasurers Report was read — Bal on hand Jan 31 — 20689.88 Disbursements for the month $8744.65 Bal on hand Feb 28 — $21788.12 on motion made by Mrs Bates & sec. by Mrs. Griffith it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

Applications —

Miss Hagerty has finished her probationary time & on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs Wilson she was accepted as a regular member —

Mrs Anna Cecil age 76 — she wishes to pay 4000 & 150 — on motion made by Mrs Wilcox & sec by Mrs Griffith she was accepted —

Mr Chas Havens [?] age 90 yrs & 10 mos — he wishes to pay 3000 & 150 — on motion made by Mrs. Tufts & sec. by Mrs. Wilson he was accepted —

Mrs. Mary A. Dobler [?] age 77 — she wishes to pay 4000 & 150.00 but as she has bet. 8 & 9000 she must pay 500.00 extra on motion made by Mrs. Hoffmann & sec. by Mrs Bates she was accepted —

Wm Raleigh [?] Dalton age 67 — as he had 5000.00 he was rejected having so much money —

Joseph Hallbauer [?] age 66 — he wishes to pay $3500 & 150 he was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Griffith

Mrs. Alice A. Wheeler [?] age 70 — she wishes to pay $4000 & 150 also accepted on motion made by Mrs. Griffith & sec. by Mrs Tufts —

Mrs. A. H. Mielkey [?] age 75 — she did not appear —

Mrs. Babara Schoppenberg [?] age 66 — wishes to pay 3000 & 150 on motion made by Mrs. Hoffmann & sec. by Mrs. Bates she was accepted —

Mrs. Aberdie [?] was discussed & on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Frink [?] the Secretary was instructed to write Mrs. Aberdie [?] to say as she was not in harmony with the House she was to make arrangements to find another home by the first of May —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday April /26 —

                        Respectfully submitted
