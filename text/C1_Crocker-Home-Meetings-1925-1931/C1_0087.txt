Wednesday Oct 1-/30 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Oct 1-/30 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham [?] —

Present - Mmes. Wilson Paddock - Stevenson Tufts Dick Haven [?] Wilcox Matson Palache [?] Borda [?] Gay Whicher [?] Andrews & McWilliams

Mrs Wilson presided in Mrs Leavitts absence. —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt. Matron & Head nurses reports also read accepted & filed —

Treasurers report was read Bal on hand Aug 31- 1651.88 Disbursements for the month 7800.04 [?] Bal on hand Sept 30/30 $1351.84 — on motion made by Mrs Haven [?] & sec. by Mrs Matson to be referred to auditing Committee —

Application —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache [?] & sec. by Mrs Whicher [?] - Miss Reedy will finish her probation Sept. 11 - she is to be accepted at the expiration of her probation —

Mrs Martha Peck is also to be accepted on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Borda [?] —

Mrs Marion Fraser on motion made by Mrs Borda [?] & sec. by Mrs. Gay was accepted to become a regular member —

On motion made by Andrews & sec. by Mrs Matson varnish is to be purchased to be applied on the linoleum in the dining room & lobby to be used under the supervision of Mrs Matson & Mrs Stump [?] to be done immediately the cost for the varnish to be $80.25 [?] —

On motion made by Mrs Dick & sec. by Mrs Tufts the room Committee to be given power to act as they see fit to renovate rooms & carpets.

The disposal of old furniture in the basement to be left to Mrs Stump [?] also the renovating of the basement as she thinks best — on motion made by Mrs Whicher [?] & sec. by Mrs. Dick —

Room 403 formerly occupied by Mrs Lindemann [?], on motion made by Mrs Haven [?] & sec. by Mrs Paddock the price for that room to be left to the discretion of the room Committee — & to be offered to Mrs Griffith [?] in the Home as she had asked for a larger room. —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Nov. 5-/30 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
