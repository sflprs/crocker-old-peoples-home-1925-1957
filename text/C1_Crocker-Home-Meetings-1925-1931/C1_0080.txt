Wednesday [struck through: April] May 7-/30 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday May 7-/30 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham —

Present Mmes Dick Hodgen [?] Palache Holden Stevensen Paddock Matson Tufts Andrews Wilson Leavitt & McWilliams

The [struck through: Secty's] Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt. Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read accepted & filed —

Treasurers report was read - Bal. on hand $2455.70 Disbursements for the month $7283.06 Bal on hand April 30/30 - $2667.64 on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Leavitt it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee - The report for March /30 was found to be correct —

Applications —

Mrs Jennie L. Katzenstein applied again - her first application was made in Oct /27 - but was not of age — she must now make out a new application, & must pay according to the present rate on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Leavitt —

Miss Rebecca Philippson [?] age 68 — she wishes to pay 4000 & 150.00 on motion made by Mrs Tufts & sec. by Mrs Leavitt.

Mrs Sadie C. Kell [?] age 68 - she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Hodgen [?] - she wishes to pay $4500 & 150.00 & has chosen room 222 —

Mr. G. C. De Ry [?] age 67 - on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Holden he was rejected owing to his physical condition & doctors advisement [?] not to accept him —

Elizabeth Moynihan age 70 — she was accepted on motion by Mrs Matson & sec. by Mrs Paddock providing the doctors certificate be satisfactory & she gives a true statement of her finances

Mrs Frances Atkinson [?] has finished her probation & on motion made by Mrs Holden & sec. by Mrs. Matson she was accepted as a regular member of the Home —

Mrs Andrews proposed two members to become associate members of the Home namely Mrs George Guy & Mrs John Wheeler [?] on motion made by Mrs Tufts & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt they were accepted — On motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Matson they were accepted to become active members of the Board of Managers —

A letter & a bill from Dr. C. W. Benjamin who questions the payment of his mothers funeral expenses to be sent to Mr. Flaherty our Atty. — on motion made by Mrs. Leavitt & sec. by Mrs. Holden —

A letter of thanks to Mrs Pedrini [?] for lemons kindly donated motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Hodgen [?] —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday June 5 [?] /30

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
