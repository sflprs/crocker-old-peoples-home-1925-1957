Wednesday May 22-/30 —
Special Meeting —

Present Mmes. Leavitt Dick Hodgen [?] Guy Wheeler [?] Wilson Matson Wilcox Stevensen Paddock Andrews & McWilliams. —

Mrs Leavitt proposed that honorary mention for Mrs Dick be spread upon the minutes therefore the following resolution was adopted —

Know all men by these present that whereas Mrs Wm. A. Dick has served the Board of Lady Managers of the Old Peoples Home as President for the past five years and whereas she has given to the Home her best endeavor & constant attention for the period of forty-nine years just past, and whereas it is the desire of the present Board of Lady Managers to properly express their appreciation of her labors therefore be it, Resolved that this recognition of her faithful service in the interests of the Home unanimously adopted by the Board in regular meeting be spread upon the minutes of that meeting as a permanent record of achievement

On motion made by Mrs Matson & sec. by Mrs Andrews the Supt. is to see all applicants who are to be accepted for membership & must be given all information necessary before being presented to the Board of Managers —

A letter is to be written to the Trustees giving all rates of admission of various priced rooms —

On motion made by Mrs Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs Wilson the Supt. is to have authority to have all printing matter done of applications & circulars —

On motion made by Mrs Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs Andrews the Supt. is to have supreme authority for all internal arrangements of the Home, & also to reaffirm the authority given the Supt. in the rules & regulations of the duties of the Supt. —

All officers signed the card for the bank authorizing the signing of checks when so required —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams Secty —
