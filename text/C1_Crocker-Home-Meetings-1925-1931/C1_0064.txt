Wednesday Feb 6-/29 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Feb. 6-/29- Present Mmes- Dick Hodgen [?] Palache Andrews. Paddock Stephenson Tufts Holden Wilson [struck through: Pratt [?]] Matson Haven Borda Wilcox Plume [?] & McWilliams —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read accepted & filed

Treasurers report was also read - Bal on hand Dec 31st $45282.56 Disbursements for the month, 12499.82 Bal. on hand Jan 31-/29 - $50928.49 Savings Acct $23852.94 [?] Checking acct $27075.55 It was accepted on motion made by Mrs Borda & sec by Mrs. Holden & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

Mrs May Smith [?] & Mrs Grace Riddock [?] on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt they are to be accepted as regular members when they have finished their probation —

Applications —

Miss Lunette [?] Ready [?] age 65- she wishes to pay $3000 & 150 & 1000 @ 4% for extra care she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs Andrews —

Miss Mary Jane Sykes age 67 — she wishes to pay 4000 & 150.00 & 1000.00 @ 4% for special care on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Palache she was accepted —

On motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Matson the Home is to care for the nurses maids & mens departments & new linoleum be bought for the nurses & maids rooms.

On motion made by Mrs Tufts & sec. by Mrs Leavitt, an electric heated steam table & food trucks, at a cost of 133.00 is to be furnished for the hospital food & trays —

Mr. Alden [?] who left the Home a few years ago wishes to return on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec by Mrs Tufts the Superintendent is to write a letter to Mr. W. O. Williamson with whom he has been living that he may return —

A motion was made by Mrs Leavitt & sec. by Mrs Tufts that the nurse & matron are to observe the rule for ordering requisitions & it is to be rigidly enforced —

A motion was made by Mrs. Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs. Plume [?], order books in duplicate are to be furnished the nurse in order that she can make her orders daily to the matron —

Mr. Hall [?] to be told to order the new ice plant & to use his own judgement, in replacing the ammonia tank — on motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Haven —

On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Hodgen [?] to call in an expert physician to give us a written diagnosis in the cases of Mrs Ross & Mrs Ahlborn [?] —

On motion made by Mrs Tufts & sec. by Mrs Plume [?] 75.00 a month is to be charged Mrs Cowden [?] for the time she has been here & the balance of her admission be returned to her without interest —
