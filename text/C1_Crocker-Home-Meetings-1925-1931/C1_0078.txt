Wednesday March 5-/30 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday March 5-/30 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham —

Present Mmes. Dick Leavitt Matson Wilson Stevenson Paddock Andrews Tufts Walkup [?] - Haven

Secty's report was read accepted & filed —

Supts. Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read & accepted —

Treasurers report read - Bal on hand Jan 31-/30 - $2488.87 Disbursements for the month $8216.31 Bal on hand Feb. 28/30 $2442.93 On motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Stevenson it was accepted & to be referred to auditing Committee

Application

Mrs. Mary V. Cunningham, Herman Anderson, Augusta M. Triebel [?] & Mr. & Mrs. Rummel [?] have finished their probation, on motion made by Mrs. Tufts & sec. by Mrs Mrs. Haven they were accepted as regular members —

Mrs. Francis Augusta Aitkison [?] age 65 — on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Tufts she was accepted she wishes to pay $5000.00 & $150.00 she has chosen Room 207 -

Philip D. Nicholson age 74 - there was no action taken as there was no doctors report —

On motion made by Mrs Walkup [?] & sec. by Mrs Wilson an additional $25.00 be given Mrs Poggon [?] the secretary in the office making her salary $100.00

A new Singer sewing machine was suggested to be bought. Mrs Stump [?] was given authority to do so —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday April 2-/30 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
