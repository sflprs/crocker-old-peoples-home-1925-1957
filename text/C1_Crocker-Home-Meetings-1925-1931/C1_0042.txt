Wednesday Sept. 7-/27 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday Sept 7-/27 — Present Mmes. Dick Hoffmann Haven Wilcox Borda Holden Griffith Blum [?] Andrews Tufts Frink & McWilliams — Secty's report was read & accepted — Treasurers report also read & on motion made by Mrs Borda & sec. by Mrs Wilcox it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing committee — Supt's & Matrons reports also read

On motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Griffith, Mr. Temple who came in last month is to be dismissed from the House at the end of his probation —

A private house telephone appliance is to be installed between the matrons quarters & the kitchen —

Applications —

Chas O. Stromberg [?] age 81 — wishes to pay $3000 & $150, Mrs J. A. Stromberg [?] age 81 — also wishes to pay $3000 & $150.00 they did not appear before the Board —

Mrs Isabelle J. Porter [?] age 71 — came into the Home since last meeting, & paid $5150.00 [?] was admitted by the Committee

Mrs. E. S. Pettibone [?] age 70 — wishes to pay 4 or 5000 — decided on the 5000.00 room but must sell property before coming in — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Haven & sec. by Mrs Griffith —

    Frank Victor [?] Jenkins [?] age 65
    Hannah Sloan [?] age 64+
    Mrs. Melvina [?] Hammond [?] 64+
        } did not appear —

A letter came from John McGuire who applied last month wishing to come in for 2500 & 150.00 — on motion made by Mrs Hoffmann & sec. by Mrs Frink a letter is to be written telling him it is against the rules & regulations of the Home to accept any one not having the required amounts

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Oct. 5-/27 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
