Wednesday June 6-/28 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday June 6-/28 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] —

Present. Mmes. Dick Hodgen Hoffmann Griffith Tufts [?] Wilson Holden [?] Andrews Haven Walkup [?] Frink & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed —

Treasurers report was read. Bal. on hand April 30. $9325.41 Disbursements for the month 7467.68 [?] Bal. on hand May 31-/28 $19677.55 [?] The auditing Committee found the report of last month to be correct — on motion made by Mrs. Petersen & sec. by Mrs. Haven it was accepted —

On motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Griffith a letter is to be written to Mrs. Corden, she must mind the rules & regulations of the Home & not annoy [?] the employees — otherwise the Board has the power, at its own discretion to ask her to leave the Home —

Applications

Mrs. Helen A. Griffin finished her probation & accepted on motion made by Mrs Griffith & sec. by Mrs. Tufts [?] —

Mrs Minnie Seale [?] age 68 — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Frink pending a result of her finances — Mrs Hodgen was appointed to see her Attorney Mrs Henry [?] Braudenstein [?]

Mrs Louise H [?] Betts [?] age 69 — she wishes to pay 3500 & 150 & will pay 1000.00 for special care at 4% — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Frink —

On motion made by Mrs Tufts [?] & sec. by Mrs. Frink she is to be permitted to come in again Sept 1-/28 to finish her probation but with the understanding that in the event her arrangements not being completed, [struck through: 150.00] & she does not become a member of the Home 150.00 will be deducted from the 3000.00 already paid in —

On motion made by Mrs. Petersen & sec. by Mrs. Hodgen the resignation of Dr. Walter Morrill [?] be accepted with regret to take effect July 1-/28 —

At the request of the Trustees, the money received monthly of 150.00 toward salaries hereafter to be paid Jan 1 & July 1, Mr Murphy to be notified to that effect — on motion made by Mrs. [struck through: Hodgen] & sec. by Mrs. Frink

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Aug /28 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
