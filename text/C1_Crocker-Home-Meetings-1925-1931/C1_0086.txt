Sept 3-/30 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Sept 3-/30 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham [?] -

Present Mmes. Leavitt Andrews Palache [?] Wilcox Paddock - Bailhache [?] Holden Borda [?] Dick Haven [?] Hodgen [?] Whicher [?] & McWilliams

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read accepted & filed

Treasurers report was read - Bal on hand July 31-/30 - $2218.20 Disbursements for the month $8666.82 Bal on hand Aug 31-/30 $1651.88 It was accepted on motion made by Mrs Palache [?] & sec. by Mrs. Holden & to be referred to the auditing Committee -

Application -

Mrs. Johanna Healy [?] age 72 - she wishes to pay $4000 & 150.00 she did not appear —

Miss Barr [?] the head nurse has asked for a four weeks vacation which was granted on motion made by Mrs Dick & sec by Mrs Paddock — 2 weeks to be on her own -

On motion made by Mrs Haven [?] & sec. by Mrs Borda [?] the Supt. is to be given power & be permitted to purchase any liquor required, & to be in accordance with the prohibition regulations.

Bids for the printing of the circulars & cards for the Home to be obtained from the Sequoia Press & Walter N. Brunt Mrs Borda [?] offered to take care of the matter — motion made by Mrs. Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs. Wilson [?] —

The Supt is to be appointed to take charge of the Manning estate & represent the Board at the probation of Mrs. Mannings will - on motion made by Mrs. Palache [?] & sec. by Mrs. Holden —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Oct. 1/30 -

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams —
