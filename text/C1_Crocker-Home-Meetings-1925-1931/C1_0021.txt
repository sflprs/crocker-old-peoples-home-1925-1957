Wednesday May 5 —/26 —

On a motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Tufts, the Board wishes to recommend the reduction [?] of the admissions committee & to be brought up at the Annual meeting & to have the change in the By-Laws —

It was decided to buy one dozen new coffee pots as the ones purchased several months ago were unsatisfactory —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday June 2 — /26 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        A. M. McWilliams —
