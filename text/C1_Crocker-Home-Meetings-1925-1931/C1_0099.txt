May. 6th 1931.

The regular meeting of the Board of Lady Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held in the Board room at. 10:30 a.m. on Wednesday. May 6th. Mr Dunham opened the meeting with a prayer. Mrs Leavitt. The President, presiding.

Mrs McWilliams. The Secretary, being absent Mrs Leavitt appointed Mrs Borda to act as Secretary. pro. tem.

[In margin: Attendance]

Those present were Mesdames: Wicher, Palache, Wilson Gay, Hodgen, Matson, Andrews, Tufts, Wilcox, Paddock, Haven, Walkup, Dick, Stevenson, Holden and Borda.

The minutes of the last meeting were read and approved.

[In margin: Reports.]

The reports of the Superintendent, Matron, and head Nurse were read and placed on file -

The Treasurer's report showed:

    Balance on Hand. March. 31st. 1931.    $3238.14.
    Disbursements for the Month.    6330.77.
    Balance on Hand April 30th 1931.    4407.37.

Mrs Wicher moved. and Mrs Gay seconded that the Treasurer's report be accepted and referred to the Auditing Committee - Motion carried -

[In margin: Acceptance]

Mrs Andrews moved and Mrs Matson seconded that the following members who had completed their two months probation be accepted as residents of the Home. Motion carried -

    Miss Marie MacKenzie
    Mrs Martha Fahey.
    Mrs Anna F. Reel.
    Mrs Elza Benham.
    Mr James G. Miller -

[In margin: Applications]

Mrs Robert Dulac of #90 Wawona Street, San Francisco, Age 65 years. came before the Board to apply for membership. Dr. Rummell [?] recommended she be accepted. She wishes to pay $4000.00 and $150.00 Probation fee. Mrs Tufts moved and Mrs Haven seconded that Mrs Dulac be accepted as a Probationary member of the Home. Motion carried -

[In margin: New Business.]

The Hospital Committee suggested that the members of the Board do a little more visiting among the sick as visits were greatly appreciated by the [rest of the last line cut off at the bottom of the photo]
