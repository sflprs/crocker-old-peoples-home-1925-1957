Wednesday Sept 5-/28 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples House was held Wednesday Sept 5-/28 —

Present Mmes Dick Hodgen Andrews Tufts [?] Palache Wilson Pritchard Borda Holden Haven Wilcox & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Matrons & Head Nurses reports also read accepted & filed —

Treasurers report was read Bal on hand July 31-/28 — $30226.54 Disbursements for the month $6987.59 [?] Bal on hand Aug 31-/28 — 35063.20 [?] auditors report for last month was found to be correct.

Applications —

Miss May Wallace age 69 — wishes to pay 3000 & 150 probation [?] she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Holden & sec. by Mrs. Haven.

Miss Bridget McNally [?] age 69 — on motion made by Mrs. Holden & sec. by Mrs. Palache she was accepted — she wishes a 3000 room & 150 probation & will pay 1000.00 @ 4% to pay for extra care.

Miss Augusta [?] Schroder [?] age 65 — she wished to pay 3500 & 150 she came before the Board but could not decide on the extra 1000 for special care. she left to give it more thought —

Miss Nellie Mulcare [?] age 68 — wishes to pay 3000 & 150 — her niece Mrs. Edgar [?] Gleason [?] of Mill Valley will be responsible for extra nursing & care — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Tufts [?] —

Addison Wilmurt [?] age 68 — he was not accepted on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Borda — he has not lived in Calif. 5 years —

Ludwig Carlson age 65 — he wishes to pay 4150.00 he did not appear

On motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs Wilson, a trained graduate nurse is to be engaged to take complete charge of the night work, & to be responsible to the Head Nurse —

Secty is to write to Mrs Wilson assistant nurse telling her of the change of plans in as much as the House has grown — & would like to place her on the day staff if agreeable to her & to have her there on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Andrews —

The officers of the Board are to try & as soon as possible to secure some one who is to fill the Superintendents position on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Andrews —

On motion made by Mrs Hodgen & sec. by Mrs Haven, Mrs. Hump [?] is to have the privilege of asking the Secty to assist her in any way she sees fit —

Mrs C. O. [?] Stephenson Jr. has been suggested as an associate member also to become a member of the Board of Managers — Mrs. Joseph Sifes [?] is also to be asked to become an associate member — On motion made by Mrs Tufts [?] & sec. by Mrs. Haven —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Oct. /28 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
