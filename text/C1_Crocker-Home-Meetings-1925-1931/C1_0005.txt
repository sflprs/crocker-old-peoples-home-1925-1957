Present Mmes — Dick Palache Hodgen Hoffmann Wilson Griffith Holden Borda [?] Bates Judson Tufts Haven Wilcox Rossier [?] Andrews Plume [?] Petersen Stone Frink [?] & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted

Treasurers report was also read — Bal on hand May 31 — $15296.19 — Disbursements for the month of June $7342.65 Bal. on hand June 31 — $13797.00 — Disbursements for the month $6578.89 Bal at end of month $21744.25 — on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Wilson it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee
