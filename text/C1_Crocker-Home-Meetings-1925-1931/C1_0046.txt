Wednesday Dec 7-/27-

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday Dec 7-/27

Present Mmes — Dick Hodgen Wilson Palache Griffith Wilcox Tufts [?] Andrews Stone [?] Borda Holden Plume [?] Frink & McWilliams

Secty's report was read accepted & filed

Supt. Matrons & nurse's reports were also read & accepted —

Treasurers report was also read Bal on hand Oct. 31-/27 $13402.35 [?] Disbursements for the month $11017.43 including the rebate of 300.00 to Mrs. Higgins Bal on hand Nov 30-/27 $14519.74 [?]

On motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Holden the case of the death of Mr. H. Young who was knocked down by a taxi cab be dropped as far as the coroners jury is concerned —

Mrs Hodgen made the motion & sec by Mrs. Palache the evidence of the head nurse, & that of Dr. Morrill [?] as regards this case be given the Board in written form —

Applications —

Mrs. Nellie Lane & Mrs. Elizabeth Corden [?] have finished their probation [?] & on motion made by Mrs. Griffith & sec. by Mrs. Tufts [?] they were accepted as regular members —

Olive [?] O Bond age 65 — she wishes to pay $3000 & 150.00 she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec by Mrs Plume [?].

Mrs. Anna J. Hague [?] age 67 — on motion made by Mrs Tufts [?] & sec. by Mrs. Frink she was accepted providing she gives $500.00 extra for future nursing as the physicians report was not very satisfactory to accept her — & she is to receive savings bank int. on the $500 — for her life time —

On motion made by Mrs. Frink & sec. by Mrs. Griffith in the future only two guests be allowed at a meal owing to the limited capacity in the dining room & also sufficient notice be given the Matron —

Dr Morrill [?] is to be notified that a Xmas box is to be placed in the office & all money given by the members of the House for the employees must be dropped in the box & no gifts of money to be accepted by any employee only through the box —

On motion made by Mrs Plume [?] & sec. by Mrs Hodgen —

Mrs Holland [?] has agreed to turn over 200.00 of the annuity she receives to the Board of Managers to be used for her care & for payment of her funeral, at the motion [struck through: suggestion] of Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Hodgen carried

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Jan 4-/28

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
