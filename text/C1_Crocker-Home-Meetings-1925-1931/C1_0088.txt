Wednesday Nov 5-/30 -

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old People Home was held Wednesday Nov 5-/30 & The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham [?]

Present Mmes. Leavitt Paddock Dick Palache [?] Hodgen [?] Matson Borda [?] Walkup [?] Wilson Andrews Tufts Wilcox Gay Whicher [?] Wilcox Haven [?] Stevenson & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supts Matrons & Head Nurses reports read & accepted on motion made by Mrs Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs Tufts —

Treasurers report also read Bal on hand Sept 30/30 - 1351.84 Disbursements for the month 7137.28 Bal. on hand Oct 31-/30 - 1714.56 on motion by Mrs Matson & sec. by Mrs Walkup [?] it was accepted - The auditing committee found the reports of August & Sept. to be correct -

On motion made by Mrs Matson & sec by Mrs Borda [?] a change in the meat list was recommended for trial - the motion was lost. —

On motion made by Mrs Hodgen [?] & sec by Mrs Wilson Mrs Stump [?] to be asked to supervise more closely the quantity quality & the serving of the meals & to be examined —

A record is to be kept of all daily requisitions in writing & be put in the hands of the matron on motion made by Mrs Palache [?] & sec. by Mrs Hodgen [?] —

Applications —

Mrs Effie Granberry [?] age 72 — she wishes to pay $4500.00 She was accepted on motion made by Mrs Palache [?] & sec by Mrs Tufts

Mrs. Etta Coffile [?] age 73 - she wishes to pay $4150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Palache [?] & sec. by Mrs Whicher [?] she was accepted

Christian Jacob Tehnich [?] age 85 — he was accepted but pending his financial statement on motion made by Andrews & sec by Mrs Paddock & Mrs Wilson interviewed him after he had left the Board room & returned with his 4 bank books value of which will be $5300.00 - Jan 1st /31 with added interest - this amount he gives [struck through: to the Home] for his entrance fee & extra care —

Mr. Paul Herz [?] - age 66 — on motion by Mrs Tufts & sec by Mrs Palache [?] he was rejected - owing to medical advice -

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs Wilcox Mrs Martignon [?] is to sign a contract & give a promissory note for $1000.00 for her special care —

On motion made by Mrs Palache [?] & sec. by Mrs Matson should any employees in the House become ill they are to be taken care of but on the third day to be removed to outside care & also left to the discretion of the President unless she deems otherwise

A motion was made by Mrs Matson & sec by Mrs Hodgen [?]
