Wednesday Oct 3-/28 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Oct 3-/28

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Palache Holden Haven Tufts [?] Stephenson Wilson Leavitt Andrews Plume [?] & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Matron's & Head Nurse's report also read accepted & filed —

Treasurer's report also read Bal on hand 35063.26 [?] Aug 31-/28 Disbursements for the month $8940.00 [?] Bal on hand Sept. 30/28 — $40091.88 [?] $28853.47 [?] & $11325.64 [?] savings acct. —

The following have finished their probation & on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs Haven they were accepted —

    Miss Seale [?] Mrs Frankel [?] Mr. Swanson [?] & Mr. Kelly

On motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs Holden the nurses on the 2nd 3rd & 4th floors are to receive 50.00 a month — In the hospital ward 55.00 per month —

On motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Plume [?] [struck through: is to] the night watchman is to be raised 5.00 making his salary 65.00 —

The laundress Mrs Jackman [?] is to have her salary raised 5.00 making it 50.00 on motion made by Mrs Holden & sec. by Mrs Leavitt —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Hodgen the raise in salary of Paul the head waiter is to lie [?] on the table for a month & the dining room & kitchen Committee is to investigate & have a talk with Mrs. Stump [?] [struck through: made a motion] Mrs Haven & sec. by Mrs Wilson later [?] [struck through: to raise his salary 5.00 [?]]

The Secretary is to write Mrs. Cleveland thanking her for the money donated to the Home in the name of Miss Zabriskie on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt —

Secretary is also to write Mrs. Bertha [?] Wilmurt [?] wife of Addison Wilmurt [?] in answer to her letter — The Board has decided not to accept her husband, & to suggest the Home in Tacoma, as it will be 5 [?] years before he could be admitted & better look elsewhere for a Home.

Applications — Mrs. Pedersen offered insurance policy not accepted —

Frank Wm Johnson age 72 — he wishes to pay 4000 & 150.00 & 1000.00 for extra care & to receive 4% — he was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Andrews —

Mrs Jennie Alice Gelmer [?] age 66 — she wishes to pay 3150.00 & was accepted on motion made by Mrs Haven & sec by Mrs Holden.

Miss Jennie G. McGee age 68 — she wishes to pay 3150.00 & 1000.00 for extra care at 4% on motion made by Mrs Tufts [?] & sec. by Mrs. Plume [?] she was accepted —

Mrs. Amelia Munson [?] age 68 — she hadn't sufficient funds — could not be considered —

Miss E. Francis Condit [?] age 75 — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Haven — on condition that her sister would pay the amount for her admission — 4000.00 for her admission, $150.00 probation & $1000.00 at 4% for extra care — totalling $5150.00
