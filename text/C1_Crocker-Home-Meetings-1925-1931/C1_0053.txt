Wednesday May 9-/28 —
Annual Meeting —

The annual meeting of the Board of Trustees & Board of Managers was held Wednesday May 9-/28 —

Present Messrs. Petersen Stephenson [?] Dr Perry [?] & Mr. Burling [?] Mmes. Dick Hodgen Palache Matson Andrews Walkup [?] Griffith

Election of the new Board of Managers & Officers took place —

On motion made by Mrs. Matson & sec. by Mrs Griffith a letter to be written Dr Morrill [?] to explain his absence from the House on this day —

The application of Eva J. Waggoner [?] was accepted & to pay 3000 & 150 & the 500.00 for special care — she was accepted by Mrs Griffith & sec. by Mrs. Frink when she had the necessary funds —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt the Secty was notified to communicate with Mrs Petersen regarding the coming of Mr. & Mrs. Clark who only paid in 300.00 [?] instead of the 1000.00 which was to be paid when they came into the House & balance to be paid later further action is in the hands of the President & if necessary the Board will uphold any action taken by them —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
