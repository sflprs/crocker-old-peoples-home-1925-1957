Wednesday Sept 4-/29 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old People Home was held Wednesday Sept 4-/29 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Dunham —

Present Mmes Dick Hodgen [?] Tufts Stevenson - Holden Palache Wilson Andrews Leavitt Paddock Haven & McWilliams

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supts Matrons & Head Nurses reports read accepted & filed.

Treasurers report was read Bal. on hand July 31-/29 - 26298.03 Disbursements for the month $9506.11 Bal on hand Aug 31-/29 - 24762.87 Checking Acct. $1956.09 Savings acct 22806.18 On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Stevenson & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

Applications —

Mrs. Nellie Agnes Stevens [?] 64 - on motion made by Mrs Tufts & sec. by Mrs. Andrews, she was accepted & will pay 65.00 a month for board for ten months & then will be eligible to become regular member She will pay 4150 - 1000 Extra care. 650.00 Board & 125.00 Funeral Ex —

Mrs. Ella May Greenlees [?] age 72 - she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Haven & sec. by Mrs. Paddock — A motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs Haven her room will be furnished for her — 3000.00 1000.00 Special care & 150.00 probation

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen [?] & sec. by Mrs. Tufts the question of the new room for the nurse be left with the advisory board with power to proceed as they think proper.

A letter is to be written by the secretary of the Board to the Board of Trustees explaining the situation & condition of the elevator, & to give it their attention on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt.

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Oct. 2-/29 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty —
