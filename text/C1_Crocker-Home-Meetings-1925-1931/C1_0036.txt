Wednesday March 2-/27 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday March 2-/27 — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] — Present Mmes. — Dick Hodgen Griffith, Hoffmann, Wilson, Rossier [?] Petersen Blum [?] Palache Leavitt Matson Frink & McWilliams Secty's report was read & accepted — Supt's & Matrons reports also read accepted & filed — Treasurers report was read — Bal on hand Jan 31-/27 — $15100.38 Disbursements for the month 6742.06 [?] Bal on hand Feb 28-/27 — $17334.06 [?] on motion made by Mrs Hodgen & sec. by Mrs Wilson it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee — The report from last month was found to be correct —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Griffith — Dr. Morrill [?] is to investigate the matter of taking some one out on parole from an asylum —

On motion made by Mrs Hodgen & sec. by Mrs Wilson — Dr. Rummell [?] is to give Mrs. Trist a thorough examination when she returns — Dr. Rummell [?] to be informed — she is also to complete her probation on her return —

Mrs Wilson withdrew her second to Mrs. Tufts motion at last meeting, rescinding the acceptance of Mrs. Trist as a permanent member — she is ill in Oakland at the present time —

Dr. Morrill [?] is to notified that the gas plates are to be readjusted & put into condition so that they may be used again —

Applications

Mrs. A. Elson [?] & Mrs. E. Morrison will have finished their probation this month — Mrs Elson [?] the 10th & Mrs Morrison the 19th on motion made by Mrs Matson & sec. by Mrs. Hoffmann they are to be accepted as regular members —

Mrs. Maria Simmons [?] age 73 she was not accepted as she did not meet with the requirements

Josephine Jacobsen age 65 — wishes to pay $4000 & 150.00 she was told she must have a doctors certificate & to return next month —

Miss Riley [?] the head nurse tendered her resignation on motion made by Mrs Wilson & sec. by Mrs Frink it was accepted with much regret —

On motion made by Mrs. Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Rossier [?] that the head nurse hereafter is to be employed by the Pres. Supt. & Hospital Committee & to cooperate in the choice of the new head nurse —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt the affairs of Mr & Mrs Lang [?] are to be placed in Mrs Hanlon's [?] hands to attend to

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday April 6/27 —
