Wednesday Feb 2-/27 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Feb. 2-/27 — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Palache Griffith Wilcox Tufts Leavitt Haven Borda Holden Matson Wilson Rossier [?] Bates Stone & McWilliams Secty's report was read & accepted Supt's & Matrons report also read accepted & filed — Treasurers report was read — Bal. on hand [struck through: Jan] Dec. 31-/27 7009.56 [?] Disbursements for the month $7849.16 Bal. on hand Jan 31-/27 $15100.38 [?] — on motion made by Mrs. Borda & sec. by Mrs. Griffith it is to be submitted to the auditing committee —

The auditing report from last month was found to be correct.

Applications — Mrs. L. Trist [?] will finish her probation Feb 13th & Miss M. McBride will finish hers Feb. 18 — on motion made by Mrs. Tufts & sec. by Mrs Wilson they were accepted as regular members —

New Applications

Helen Emeline Healy age 75 — application was read last month also she appeared before the Board but was not accepted — as her physical condition was not up to the standard required —

Mr. Joseph Jennings age 70 — as there was no room for him could not be accepted —

Mr. Booth who takes charge of the garden is to be given at least 5.00 for garden supplies — on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Holden —

On motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Wilson a committee to be appointed to renew carpets & furnishings in the Chapel — Mrs McWilliams Griffith & Rossier [?] were appointed.

On motion made Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs. Tufts a sign is to posted over each gas plate that the Board of Managers had passed a resolution that it was to be used only for water tea coffee milk cocoa toast & boiled & poached eggs — If this rule is violated the gas plate will be disconnected.

All suggestions & duties for the head nurse made by the committee at a meeting held Jan 10 — be accepted on motion made by Mrs. Matson & sec. by Mrs Bates —

It was also decided by the Board that all special nurses are to be engaged by the Home even tho' an outside physician is in charge —

On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Wilcox that it is to be a rule of the House that there must be either the Supt. or the Engineer & the Matron or the head nurse remain in the House so that there will always be two responsible heads parties at the same time for the good & safety of the House.

On motion made by Mrs. Wilson & sec. by Mrs. Wilcox each of the heads of the House to have a week end vacation each month —
