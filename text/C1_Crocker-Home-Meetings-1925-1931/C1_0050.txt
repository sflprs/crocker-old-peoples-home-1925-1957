Wednesday March 7-/28 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday March 7-/28 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] —

Present Mmes. Dick Hodgen Palache Tufts [?] Wilcox Griffith Andrews Frink Leavitt Rossier [?] Matson McWilliams

Secty's report was read accepted & filed —

Supt's & Head nurses reports also read accepted & filed

Mrs Shimp [?] being away & ill there was no Matrons report —

Treasurers report was read Bal on hand Jan 31-/28 — $14460.39 Disbursements for the month 8448.90 [?] Bal on hand Feb. 29. — $10474.56

Applications —

Mrs Olive Bond Miss Nellie Vaughan [?] & Mrs. Anna Hague [?] have finished their probation on motion made by Mrs Palache & sec. by Mrs. Griffith, they were accepted as regular members of the House —

W. J. Moon [?] age 75 — he wishes to pay $3000 & 150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Frink & sec. by Mrs. Andrews he was accepted.

Mrs. Ella J. Chamberlain age 76 — she wished to pay 3650.00 on motion made by Mrs Andrews & sec. by Mrs Tufts [?] she was rejected as the physicians report was very unsatisfactory —

Mrs. Maude [?] Wall [?] age 68 — she wishes to pay $3150.00 as her finances were not available at the present time — she will return next month —

Mrs. Emma [?] V. Matignon [?] age 69 — wishes to pay $3150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Wilcox & sec. by Mrs. Frink she was accepted — $500.00 extra for special care —

Mrs. Julia Neuebaum [?] age 64 + — wishes to pay $5000.00 & 150.00 she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Tufts [?] & sec. by Mrs. Griffith. she will pay 75.00 a month board until of age $1000.00 Bonus & $1000.00 @ 4% for special care.

Mrs. Helen A. Griffin [?] age 66 — she was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Wilcox & sec. by Mrs. Matson — she will also pay 1000.00 for special care @ 4% & will pay $4000.00 & 150.00

Mrs. Ida Meyer [?] age 66 — wishes to pay $3650.00 but did not appear —

The new local phones have been installed for the nursing Dept-

All money belonging to the [struck through: Hospital fund] & in the General fund is to be transferred to the Hospital fund on motion made by Mrs. Palache & sec. by Mrs Griffith — The special nursing for Mrs. Pump [?] is to be drawn from the Hospital fund on motion made by Mrs. Frink & sec. by Mrs. Andrews —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday April 4- /28 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty
