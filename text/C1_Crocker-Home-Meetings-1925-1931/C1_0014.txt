Wednesday Dec 2 —/25 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday Dec 2 —/25 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] —

Present Mmes. Dick Palache Petersen Wilson Hodgen Judson Holden Tufts Pritchard Wilcox Borda [?] Bates Stone Griffith Rossier [?] Frink [?] Matson Plume [?] Andrews & McWilliams —

Secty's report was read & accepted on motion made by Mrs. Tufts & sec. by Mrs. Petersen

Supt & Matrons reports also were read & accepted —

Treasurers report was read — Bal on hand Oct 31 — $24914.98 — Disbursements for the month $8305.23 Bal on hand Nov. 30 — $20099.00

On motion made by Mrs Matson & sec. by Mrs Frink [?] it is referred to the auditing committee to be accepted —

Applications —

Mrs Rebecca Sills [?] & Jacob Baker have finished their probationary time & on motion made by Mrs. Petersen & sec. by Mrs. Stone she was accepted as regular members —

Mrs. Annie Chambers Smyth [?] age 66 — does not wish to pay over 3000.00 but when interviewed would wait — & the application was laid on the table —

Mrs Bushnell was discussed & asked to come before the Board as regards her trays — it was decided on motion made by Mrs Petersen & sec. by Mrs Plume [?] she is to have her breakfast served to her & left to the discretion of the Board —

Letters of thanks are to sent — Mrs Sadie [?] Coffin [?] for her donation of wash cloths, Mrs Sutro [?] for the Radio set & for the Victrola & lamp given by Mrs. Wolcott [?] —

The Xmas decorations dinner & gifts were also discussed Mrs Griffith to attend to the purchasing of the candy, Mrs Plume [?] the wreaths & garlands —

The regulation Xmas dinner also to be had —

The gifts of money to the help are to given as in former years —

On motion made by Mrs Petersen & sec. by Mrs Bates Mr. Haylor [?] is to be consulted by the Secty in regard to Mr Lymers [?] & the taking away of the typewriter —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Jan 6 — /26 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
                             Secty [partly cut off at the bottom of the photo]
