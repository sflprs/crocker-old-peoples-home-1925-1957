Wednesday March 4/31 —

On motion made by Mrs Watson [?] & sec. by Mrs Andrews, the necessary vacuum cleaners to be supplied for the Home —

The changing of the door man to be left in the hands of the Superintendent —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday April 1-/31 —

March /31 —

A special meeting was called for the executive Committee to discuss the rearrangement of tables in the dining room as well as the painting & redecorating of same. Those present Mrs Leavitt Wilson Paddock Matson & McWilliams — Bids were to be obtained from several — & to be decided at the next meeting —
