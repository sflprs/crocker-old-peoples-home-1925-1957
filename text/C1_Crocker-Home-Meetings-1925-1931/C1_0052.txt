Wednesday May 2-/28 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers of the Old Peoples Home was held Wednesday May 2-/28 —

Present Mmes Dick Hodgen Hoffmann Palache Griffith Tufts [?] Matson Petersen Frink Andrews Haven Stone [?] Leavitt & McWilliams —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] —

Secty's report read & accepted

Supts Matrons & Head Nurses reports all read accepted & filed

Treasurers report also read — Bal. on hand March 31-/28 — $9926.03 Disbursements for the month 7134.66 [?] Bal on hand April 30. /28 — $9325.41 [?] The auditing report for the past month was found to be correct — & on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Matson was accepted.

A suggestion made by the fire department inspector of changing the windows leading to the fire escapes to doors was accepted on motion made by Mrs Hodgen & sec. by Mrs Frink —

Applications — Miss M. E. Gallagher & Mr. W. J. Moon will finish their probation in a few days [struck through: will finish their probation] & on motion made by Mrs. Tufts [?] & sec. by Mrs Haven they were accepted as regular members —

Mrs Anna M. Reimer [?] age 67 — wishes to pay $3000 & $150 but does not wish to come in for a year —

Mrs May A. Brummel [?] age 66 — wishes to pay 3000 & 150 she was accepted on motion made by Mrs Griffith & sec. by Mrs Tufts [?] she was accepted —

Mrs. Matilda C. Lods [?] age 80 — also accepted on motion made by Haven & sec. by Mrs. Griffith — she will pay $3250 [?] & 150.00

Mr. Fort [?] Gunnar [?] Clark age 74 —

Mrs Fort [?] Gunnar [?] Clark age 68 Each one to pay 3000 & 150.00 — as they do not wish to come in until Sept. 1 — but would like to secure a large room, deposit 1000.00 — but another doctors certificate when ready to come in must be given — on motion made by Mrs Hodgen & sec. by Mrs. Frink — their proposition was accepted —

Mrs Minnie [?] L. Lindberg [?] age 72 — she wished to pay $3150.00 but decided she would not come in, & would not state her finances —

Mrs. May F. Prendergast [?] age 69 — wishes to pay $3150.00 & was accepted on motion made by Mrs. Hoffmann & sec. by Mrs. Leavitt —

A list of suggestions for the Board to present to the Trustees was made up & to be read by the Secty at the Annual Meeting Wednesday May 9-/28 —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday June /28 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams —
