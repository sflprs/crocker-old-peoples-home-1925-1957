Wednesday Nov. 3 /26 —

The regular monthly meeting was held Wednesday Nov. 3 — The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?] Present Mmes. — Dick Hodgen Pritchard Tufts Wilcox Hoffmann [?] Griffith Bates Andrews Leavitt [struck through: Andrews] Matson Blum [?] Frink Stone [?] Borda & McWilliams — Secty's report was read & accepted — Supt's & Matrons reports also read, accepted & filed — Treasurer's report was read Bal on hand Sept 30-/26 — $9508.63 Disbursements for the month $7264.94 [?] Bal on hand $15654.42 [?] Oct 31-/26 — on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Matson — it was accepted & to be referred to the auditing Committee —

Applications —

Mrs Mary Dobbs [?] Miss Mary McLean Mrs Dora Petersen have finished their probationary time on motion made by Mrs Leavitt & sec. by Mrs. Tufts they were accepted as regular members —

Josephine Burmeister age 71 — she wishes to pay $4000 & $150.00 her application was laid over —

Joseph Wm Pertlecleke [?] age 65 — on motion made by Mrs. Hoffmann [?] & sec. by Mrs. Tufts he was accepted & will pay $3000 & 150.00 — would have preferred a larger room but as there was none available, will occupy one at this price but should he move to a larger room understands thoroughly he will pay $125.00 for the repainting — carpeting

Mrs R. O'Neill age 75 — wants to pay $3000 & 150.00 she did not appear —

Mrs M. A. Miller age 69 — she did not appear —

Mrs Fannie M. Burke age 65 — wishes to pay $3000.00 & $150.00

On motion made by Mrs. McWilliams & sec. by Mrs Matson a new coffee urn is to be purchased — one that has been in use only four weeks, price 185.00 the regular price for the same would be $345.00 — was decided upon —

A letter is to be written to a Mrs Holcombe, who had written to the Board of Managers asking to have fancy work displayed in all the sitting rooms — on motion made by Mrs. Frink & sec. by Mrs Bates it was decided no —

The regular Thanksgiving turkey dinner is to be served

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday Dec. 1st /26 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams —
