Wednesday April 4-/28 —

The regular monthly meeting of the Board of Managers was held Wednesday April 4-/28 —

The meeting was opened with prayer by Dr. Morrill [?]

Present Mmes. Dick, Hodgen, Griffith, Andrews, Haven, Tufts [?], Matson, Leavitt, Plume [?] Frink & McWilliams

Secty's report was read & accepted —

Supt's & Matron & Head nurses reports were also read accepted & filed

Treasurers report was read Bal. on hand Feb 29-/28 — 10474.56 Disbursements for the month 9258.51 [?] Bal. on hand March 31-/28 $9926.03 [?] The auditing Committee found the report to be correct.

Applications —

Mrs. Bourdillon [?] has finished her probationary time & on motion made by Mrs. Griffith & sec. by Mrs. Haven she was accepted as a regular member —

Mrs. Eva [?] J. Waggoner [?] age 72 — she wishes to pay $3000 & 150.00 on motion made by Mrs. Andrews & sec. by Mrs. Frink she is to return next month with a report on her financial condition & the doctors certificate —

On motion made by Mrs. Leavitt & sec. by Mrs. Matson, suggestions for provisions in the new contract be presented for discussion at our next meeting —

There being no further business the meeting adjourned to Wednesday May 2-/28 —

                        Respectfully submitted
                        Amelia [?] M. McWilliams
